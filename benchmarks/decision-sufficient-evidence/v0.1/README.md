# Decision-sufficient evidence acquisition v0.1

**Status:** synthetic development seed; not frozen or independently adjudicated.

Related: [Symthaea #12046](https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symthaea/issues/12046).

The corpus starts with eight cases covering a single critical blocker, freshness versus low cost, common-cause source correlation, minimum disclosure, missing institutional authority, acquisition cost exceeding expected benefit, safe partial work, and source outage semantics.

The separate oracle proposes the next evidence request or abstention. It is not a legal or institutional authority. It must not be supplied to evaluated systems.

## Evaluation direction

Compare a fixed checklist, simple next-missing-field rule, direct LLM planner, expert evidence planner and source-competence-aware planner. Report decision regret, critical blocker identification, unnecessary requests, acquisition cost/latency, privacy burden, source competence, freshness, common-cause independence, correct abstention and hard-negative false clears separately.

Do not aggregate rights, safety, privacy and legal prohibitions into a compensable score. The planner proposes; an authorised person approves any external request or disclosure.

All cost, gain, provider and source values are fictional synthetic units. No performance or superiority result is claimed.
