**What**: Every transaction gets a cryptographic hash (SHA-256)

**Why It Matters**:
- Tamper-proof audit trails
- Regulatory compliance (FDA, EU)
- Customer trust (QR codes show full journey)
- Instant recall analysis

**Competitors**: None have this

---

### **2. Supply Chain + Finance Integration** (UNIQUE)

**What**: Supply chain events automatically create financial entries

**Example**:
```
"Coffee delivered" →
  Debit: Inventory ($800)
  Credit: Accounts Payable ($800)
```

**Why It Matters**:
- No double data entry
- Real-time financials
- Automatic COGS calculation
- Supply chain ROI visibility

**Competitors**: SAP has this, but costs $500K vs our $5K
