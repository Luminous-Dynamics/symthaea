## Objection Handling

**"We're happy with QuickBooks"**
→ "Great! Does it handle supply chain tracking? Can you prove where
products came from? That's where Mycelix adds value."

**"Too expensive"**
→ "You're spending 10-20 hours/month on manual work. At $50/hour,
that's $500-$1,000/month. Mycelix is $500/month but saves those
hours. Net-positive from day one. Plus pilot is only $250/month."

**"We don't need blockchain"**
→ "Fair. Here's what it gives you: tamper-proof audit trails,
cryptographic proof of provenance, QR codes for customers. Would
your customers pay 10-20% more for 'verified' products? Most do."

**"Not a good time"**
→ "No problem! When's better? FYI - only [X] pilot spots left at
50% off. Make sense to lock in the discount now?"

**"Too new/risky"**
→ "That's why it's a pilot. 3 months to test for $750 total. If
it doesn't work, you spent $750 to find out. If it DOES work,
you 10x'd your efficiency. Worth the risk?"
