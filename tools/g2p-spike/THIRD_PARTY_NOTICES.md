## CMU Pronouncing Dictionary (CMUdict)

Piper Plus G2P 0.5.3 documents its English phonemizer as CMUdict-derived and its `bundled-dicts` feature embeds `cmudict_data.json`.

CMUdict upstream states that use for research or commercial purposes is unrestricted and requests that users acknowledge its origin when using or redistributing the material. It also notes that the dictionary may contain errors, omissions and inconsistencies.

Upstream project and attribution:
https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict

This spike is diagnostic. Its English pronunciation output is a candidate hypothesis, not ground truth.
