# Symtropy Roadmap: The Metabolism of a Type 1 Civilization

## North Star

Symtropy is the physical nervous system for the Mycelix ecosystem. It provides the **N-dimensional, Φ-coupled physics** and **Active Inference (FEP) agents** required to transition from extraction-based automation to sovereign, regenerative stewardship.
