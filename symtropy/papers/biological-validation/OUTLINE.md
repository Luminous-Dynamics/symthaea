# Paper 4: Biological Validation

**Title**: "Emergent Biological Scaling Laws in Integration-Coupled Multi-Agent Simulations"
**Target**: Frontiers in Computational Neuroscience
**Length**: 10-15 pages

## Abstract (draft)

Without fitting to biological data, a thermodynamic multi-agent engine reproduces two quantitative biological observations: (1) per-capita metabolic scaling with population size (β=-0.178 vs β=-0.19 in eusocial insects, Hou et al. 2010), and (2) a consciousness energy threshold at 45% of normal metabolic rate (vs 42% clinical anesthesia threshold, Stender et al. 2015). We additionally find that Helmholtz free energy (F = U - TS) predicts agent survival better than raw energy, and a phase transition in cooperation occurs at a critical maintenance pressure analogous to temperature-driven phase transitions in statistical mechanics. These emergent matches suggest the thermodynamic enforcement layer captures something real about the energy economics of integrated biological systems, independent of the specific consciousness metric used.
