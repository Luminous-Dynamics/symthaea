# Mycelix Demo Readiness — Substrate of a Type 1 Civilization

**Goal**: A live demo that doesn't feel like "a cool distributed-systems project" but like **the operating system a planetary-scale civilization actually runs on**.

**Distinguishing mark**: the viewer leaves thinking *"this is infrastructure,"* not *"this is an app."*

---

## Part 1 — What "Type 1 civ substrate" feels like

A Kardashev Type 1 civilization has harnessed planetary-scale energy. Its substrate isn't an app store — it's the ground-layer coordination system that lets 10 billion people run a world without collapse. The demo should evoke that ground-layer quality.

**Six aesthetic commitments** (these show up in every screen):

1. **Information density over sparse dashboards.** Civilizational systems show telemetry — not Instagram. Every view has at least 3 meaningful signals per panel, not a single hero number with whitespace.
2. **Temporal motion.** Data isn't frozen. Commons allocations move. Votes arrive. Ships in orbit track. Even when "nothing is happening" there's a low-level heartbeat.
3. **Zoomability across scale.** One atomic gesture (scroll / pinch) moves from individual → family → community → region → planet. The UX treats scale as a continuous variable, not discrete pages.
4. **Connections drawn, not just nodes.** When you look at a credential, you see *who endorsed it → what consortium they vouch for → which domains it unlocks*. The graph is always visible.
5. **No corporate gloss.** Typography reads as infrastructure — monospace headers, data-table rhythm, plot aesthetics. The visual language of Bloomberg + control rooms + scientific instruments, not SaaS.
6. **Cross-cluster coherence.** Every cluster's frontend shares a design-token layer. Praxis and Hearth look like organs of the same body, not two different apps that happen to be federated.

**What it is NOT**: a marketing page. Not hero images, not "Join the revolution" CTAs, not product-tour carousels. The viewer should feel like they just got dropped into the control room of a working civilization.

## Part 2 — The demo narrative (draft storyboard, ~12 minutes)

A single persona's day, threaded through 10 clusters. Not "here's cluster 1, here's cluster 2" — a *story* in which each cluster appears because it's needed for what the persona is doing.

**Persona**: *Kagiso*, 34, water-system caretaker in a bioregional commons. Holds a lineage in both Xhosa family circles (hearth) and a regional water council (civic). Her day:

| # | Moment | Clusters exercised | What the viewer sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Morning — unlock identity** | personal, identity | Sovereign-profile vault unlocks; 8D civic score visible (she's Citizen tier in 6 dimensions, Steward in 2). |
| 2 | **Check overnight proposals** | governance, civic | Regional council has 3 pending water-allocation proposals. She reviews the Epistemic Cube E-N-M classification of the claims. |
| 3 | **Cast verified vote** | governance + attribution | Votes with zkp consciousness-attestation (replay-prevention we just fixed on validate_with_freshness). Attribution cluster's stewardship score ticks up. |
| 4 | **Morning commons check** | commons (water-steward) | Watershed health dashboard: flow rate, quality signatures from 47 sensor nodes, anomaly at node 12. |
| 5 | **Dispatch investigation** | civic (emergency) + commons (transport) | Emergency coordination zome routes a field-tech van. Cross-cluster dispatch: civic → commons-transport via the wired civic_bridge. |
| 6 | **Field arrival — credential check** | praxis + craft | The field tech's praxis-issued "water-systems apprentice" credential is verified via craft_graph.list_my_published_credentials. Guild (craft) auto-approves the task scope. |
| 7 | **Work logged** | craft (work-history) + finance (TEND) | Session verified peer-to-peer. TEND (learning-credit currency) flows from the commons pool to the apprentice via finance. |
| 8 | **Kagiso's care moment** | hearth | Reminder: her grandmother's weekly care call. Hearth surfaces the rhythm. Kagiso schedules it. Gratitude bond recorded. |
| 9 | **Audit the day's flows** | observatory + attribution | Observatory dashboard shows 7 cross-cluster dispatches, TEND minted/burned balance sheet, reciprocity graph delta. |
| 10 | **Evening — orbital coordinator ping** | space | Kagiso's also a part-time debris-bounty hunter. A conjunction alert from mycelix-space. She logs a maneuver commitment; trust credential ticks up. |
| 11 | **Close the day** | music, personal | Listening to consciousness-shaped ambient generated by Muse; music plays royalty TEND flows settle overnight via finance. Sovereign profile re-signs. |

**What this narrative does**:
- Threads **10 clusters** (personal, identity, governance, civic, commons, praxis, craft, finance, hearth, attribution, observatory, space, music)
- Shows **5 cross-cluster dispatches** live
- Has a **human at the center** — not a flow chart
- Gives each cluster a specific dramatic moment (viewer remembers "the watershed anomaly," not "we used zome X")

**Missing from this narrative (deliberately)**: knowledge cluster doesn't appear as a headline — it's the substrate the Epistemic Cube calls into. Marketplace doesn't appear — that's Part 2 (or retired). Supplychain appears implicitly (water-sensor provenance).

## Part 4 — The 4 structural blockers before demo-ready

These are the things that, if not fixed, prevent *any* version of this demo from shipping:

### 1. **Finance regression** (coordination-blocked)

`finance-wire-types` on main is out of sync with the consumer crates on main; the richer shape lives in a concurrent session's worktree (`session-substrate-wiring`). Every demo flow that touches TEND, SAP, treasury, staking, or recognition is red.

**Path**: coordinate with the other session — they push, or we pull from the worktree with their blessing. Don't solo-fix (risk of divergence if they have more pending changes).

### 2. **Unified hApp install hasn't been smoke-tested end-to-end** in this session's context

`mycelix-workspace/happs/mycelix-unified-happ.yaml` lists 21 roles. No recent commit says "I installed this on a fresh conductor and got a running holon across all 21." Without that, the demo has a terrifying cliff on day-of.

**Path**: spin up a fresh Holochain conductor, run `hc app install` against the unified bundle, walk through conductor logs for any role that fails to provision, fix each. Probably 1 full focused session.

### 3. **Design system fragmentation across 27 Leptos frontends**

Each cluster's Leptos app has its own CSS. Praxis is polished. Craft is custom. Civic doesn't have a UI at all. Hearth has Bevy integration. They look like separate apps.

**Path**: create `mycelix-leptos-design` crate with shared design tokens (color, typography, motion, layout grid, component primitives — Button, Card, DataTable, TelemetryLine, GraphNode). Mandate all cluster Leptos apps use it. One week of work + adoption rollout.

### 4. **Seed data is thin or missing**

A demo with empty screens feels dead. Each cluster needs realistic seed data — not Lorem Ipsum, but something that reads as plausible infrastructure state (sensor readings from 47 nodes, voting history with real-shaped distributions, credential endorsements that look like a living web).

**Path**: seed-data generators per cluster, committed as fixtures. Probably 3-5 days, one cluster at a time.

## Part 5 — Design system direction (opinionated first draft)

### Color palette
- **Base**: near-black (`#0a0e14`) background, not pure black. Carbon, with warmth.
- **Foreground**: off-white (`#c9d1d9`), high-contrast but not glare.
- **Accent-1 — "signal"**: a specific cyan (`#00d4ff`) for live/moving data.
- **Accent-2 — "consensus"**: a gold (`#f0b72f`) for governance/verified states.
- **Accent-3 — "care"**: a warm green (`#7ee787`) for hearth/care/growth.
- **Accent-4 — "alarm"**: an amber-red (`#ff7b72`) used sparingly.

No drop shadows. No gradients except data-heat maps. No rounded corners beyond 2px.

### Typography
- **Headers**: monospace (Iosevka, JetBrains Mono, Berkeley Mono). Reads as infrastructure.
- **Body**: variable sans (Inter, Geist). Tight letter-spacing, unforgiving line-height.
- **Data**: tabular-num variant, always. Numbers must align.

### Motion
- Nothing decorative. Animation serves to show *state change* or *data arrival*.
- Arrival pulse: data enters with a 180ms cyan sweep, not a fade-up.
- No bouncy easings. `cubic-bezier(.22,.61,.36,1)` or linear for data.

### Layout primitives
- **TelemetryLine** — a thin horizontal strip showing one stream of data over time. Building block of every dashboard.
- **GraphNode** — a dot-with-label that auto-connects to its neighbors. Used for kinship, reciprocity, credentials, orbital traffic — same component, different data.
- **DataTable** — 1-line-per-row, zero padding, sort/filter inline. The backbone of lists.
- **ClusterCard** — the cross-cluster navigation primitive. Always shows cluster name + live signal density, not a logo.

### Information density rules
- Every full-screen view shows at minimum: 3 live signals, 1 graph, 1 data table, 1 cluster-context indicator.
- "Simple" single-purpose views are fine for sub-routes but never for top-level cluster pages.

## Part 6 — Sequenced execution path (4 weeks → demo ready)

**Week 1: Unblock**
- Finance regression resolved via coordination (another session's PR merged or wire-types sync'd)
- Unified hApp install end-to-end tested on fresh conductor. Fix every role that fails.
- mycelix-leptos-design crate scaffolded with color tokens + monospace typography

**Week 2: Test + polish the keepers**
- Craft: 25+ tests, `get_sovereign_credential` + credential pipeline covered
- Music: smoke tests for audio path (the `7da93ad23f` feature)
- Attribution: last 2 of 8 collectors wired + tests for the consumer-facing reciprocity fn
- Supplychain: bridge inbound contract tests (50+ total)

**Week 3: Frontend unification**
- mycelix-leptos-design crate has all primitives (TelemetryLine, GraphNode, DataTable, ClusterCard)
- Praxis adopts (reference implementation)
- 3-5 other cluster frontends migrate
- Civic gets a minimum-viable Leptos frontend (currently none)

**Week 4: Demo prep**
- Seed data generators for every demo-path cluster
- Full narrative walkthrough rehearsed against real running conductor
- Telemetry recording (what actually happened, for post-demo technical writeup)
- Backup/offline fallback plan

## Part 8 — What this doc does NOT attempt

- **Does not retire anything.** User direction is "keep and improve everything" — marketplace stays in the product-gate decision queue but isn't proposed for retirement here.
- **Does not finalize the design system.** The Part 5 choices are an opinionated starting draft, not a decision. The DRI (Part 7 item 2) makes the final calls.
- **Does not write the demo script.** Part 2's storyboard is a skeleton, not the speaker's text. That's a day of writing with a human-facing voice (the user's).
- **Does not estimate headcount.** "4 weeks → demo-ready" assumes current session velocity + 1 person picking up each week's work, not a fresh hiring pass.
