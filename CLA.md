# Contributor License Agreement — Luminous Dynamics

## Purpose

Luminous Dynamics uses a dual-licensing model: the source code is available
under the AGPL-3.0-or-later for the commons, and separately under commercial
licenses for organizations that cannot comply with the AGPL's copyleft
requirements. To maintain the ability to offer commercial licenses, Luminous
Dynamics must have sufficient rights over all contributed code.

This CLA ensures that:
1. Your contributions remain part of the open-source commons under the AGPL.
2. Luminous Dynamics can continue to offer commercial licenses to fund
   ongoing development of the project.
3. You retain full ownership of your contributions.

**By submitting a pull request to any Luminous Dynamics repository, you
indicate your agreement to this CLA.**

No separate signing process is required. Your first pull request constitutes
acceptance. If you are contributing on behalf of an organization, the person
submitting the pull request represents that they have authority to bind the

## Why This Matters

Without a CLA, external contributions would make it legally impossible for
Luminous Dynamics to offer commercial licenses — because each contributor
would hold copyright over their lines of code, and the AGPL would be the
only available license. This would permanently close the commercial funding
path that sustains development of the project.

The CLA ensures that:
- **The commons stays open.** All contributions are always available under
  the AGPL.
- **The project stays funded.** Commercial licensing revenue funds full-time
  development.
- **Contributors keep their rights.** You own your code. You just also let
  us use it commercially.

This is the same model used by MongoDB, GitLab, Grafana, and other
dual-licensed open-source projects.
