# SCI-001 Summary

The Scientific Method Kernel audit proposes a **small shared substrate**, not a replacement for domain science.

The central architectural decision is to preserve explicit distinctions among artifact identity, observation, interpretation, claim, evidence, replication topology, scientific disposition, and action authority.

The audit recommends generalizing only recurring structural contracts such as content identity, execution identity, preregistration, evidence contribution, and dependency lineage, while keeping domain-owned semantics such as causal identification assumptions, conformal exchangeability, physical safety, and measurement validity outside the common kernel.

The immediate follow-up order is dependency-gated: content identity and execution-capsule work should not be generalized until their source qualification lines are strong enough that a common contract cannot weaken them.
