## What You Need

### Per Application:

1. **Specification (PDF)** — Your IDD is essentially this. Convert to PDF.
   - Title of invention
   - Technical field
   - Background (what problem exists)
   - Detailed description of the invention
   - How it works step-by-step
   - Alternative embodiments / variations
   - Drawings / diagrams (recommended but not required for provisionals)

2. **Cover Sheet** — USPTO Form SB/16 (Provisional Application Cover Sheet)
   - Download: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/apply/forms
   - Fill in: title, inventor name, address, correspondence info

3. **Micro Entity Certification** — Form SB/15A (gross income basis)
   - Certifies you qualify for 80% fee reduction
   - Must be filed WITH the application

4. **Filing Fee** — $65 per application (micro entity)

### What You Do NOT Need for a Provisional:

- Claims (not required — but your IDDs already have them, which is good)
- Formal drawings (informal sketches are fine)
- An attorney
- An oath/declaration

## Critical Gotchas

1. **Your IDDs already have claims** — that's actually beneficial. Even though claims aren't required for provisionals, having them strengthens your filing because they define the scope you'll later claim in the non-provisional.

2. **Be maximally detailed** — a vague provisional won't support strong non-provisional claims later. Your IDDs at 469-563 lines each are already more detailed than most provisionals.

3. **Include alternative embodiments** — mention variations (different HV dimensions, different numbers of ethical signals, alternative substrate types). This broadens your coverage.

4. **Don't forget the micro entity form** — filing without it means you pay 4x more.

5. **12-month clock starts on filing date** — you MUST file a non-provisional within 12 months or lose the priority date. No extensions.

6. **Prior art concern** — your code was in a public GitHub repo. Under US law, you have a 1-year grace period from your own publication. Internationally (PCT), this may have started the clock. Filing now preserves your US rights and strengthens any future PCT filing.
