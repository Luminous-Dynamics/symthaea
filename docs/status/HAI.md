# Technical One-Pager: HAI
## Hyperdimensional Active Inference

### The Vision
Bring sophisticated biological-style intelligence to the extreme edge. By mapping the mathematical framework of Active Inference (the Free Energy Principle) into Hyperdimensional Vector Space, we enable high-performance, low-power autonomy.

### The Innovation: Precision-Weighted Binding
Standard Active Inference requires expensive matrix-inversion and belief-updating steps.
*   **The Breakthrough:** Beliefs are represented as 16,384-dimensional hypervectors.
*   **Symbolic Uncertainty:** Precision weighting is implemented through HDC binding strength, allowing the agent to naturally gate information based on its own uncertainty.
*   **Efficiency:** Action selection (expected free energy) is computed through vector similarity searches, which can be accelerated on commodity hardware or specialized HDC ASICs.

### Benchmarks
*   **T-Maze:** Successfully solves classic active inference benchmarks with 10x lower memory overhead than standard Dirichlet-categorical models. (Status: **measured / prototype**)
*   **Energy Efficiency:** Target power consumption of <1W for full-scale cognitive cycles on embedded hardware. (Status: **target / hypothesis**)

### Applications
1.  **SAR Robotics:** Search-and-rescue drones that can navigate complex, unknown environments with limited battery.
2.  **Autonomous Sensors:** Self-diagnosing industrial sensors that can "reason" about their own substrate health.
3.  **Bipedal Control:** Highly robust humanoid motor control derived from Riemannian manifold geodesics in HDC space.
