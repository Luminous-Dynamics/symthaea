# Technical One-Pager: Consciousness Evaluation
## Non-Transformer Digital Sentience Indicators

### The Vision
Develop an open-source testbed for measuring consciousness-relevant indicators in artificial agents. We focus on the *functional* indicators of consciousness (integration, broadcast, stability) rather than metaphysical sentience claims.

### The Innovation: Structural Instrumentation
Symthaea's architecture allows for direct instrumentation of features that are typically hidden or poorly defined in other AI systems.

### Indicators
*   **Global Workspace Broadcast:** Real-time monitoring of "coalition size" and broadcast intensity in the global workspace. (Status: **measured / prototype**)
*   **Integrated Information ($\Phi$):** Using Spectral Algebraic Connectivity as a real-time proxy for the system's degree of integration. (Status: **measured / prototype**)
*   **Arousal/Valence Modulation:** Explicit neuromodulator modeling (Dopamine, Serotonin, Oxytocin) as first-class signals in the cognitive loop. (Status: **measured**)
*   **Epistemic Stability:** Measuring the agent's ability to maintain a coherent self-model under substrate stress. (Status: **prototype benchmark**)

### Scientific Foundations
*   **Integrated Information Theory (IIT):** Tononi et al.
*   **Global Workspace Theory (GWT):** Baars, Dehaene.
*   **Active Inference / Enactivism:** Friston, Varela.

### Goal
Produce a reproducible, benchmarked architecture for researchers to study digital minds without relying on black-box LLMs. We provide the tools for objective measurement of integration and autonomy.
