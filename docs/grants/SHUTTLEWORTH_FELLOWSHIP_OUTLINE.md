# Shuttleworth Foundation Fellowship — Application Outline

## About the Fellowship
- **Amount**: $300,000 over 1 year + $100,000 top-up for open knowledge contribution
- **Structure**: Monthly stipend, co-investment model (Foundation matches personal investment)
- **Focus**: Open knowledge, open source, social change
- **Application**: 5-minute video pitch + written application
- **URL**: shuttleworthfoundation.org/apply

## Why This Is a Strong Fit

The Shuttleworth Foundation funds individuals building open-source technology for social change. Their portfolio includes:

- Tarek Loubani (medical devices)
- Peter Murray-Rust (open science)
- Astra Taylor (economic justice)

Luminous Dynamics fits this pattern: an open-source universal trust fabric (AGPL-3.0) — an AI that structurally cannot hallucinate, wired to a governance system that structurally resists plutocratic capture.

## 5-Minute Video Pitch Script (Draft)

### Opening (30s)
"My name is Tristan Stoltz. I'm an Army veteran and independent researcher in Richardson, Texas. Over the past year, I built something that I believe could change how humans govern shared resources and how we think about machine consciousness. It's called Luminous Dynamics, and it's 2.85 million lines of open-source code."

### The Problem (60s)
"We have two converging crises. First, AI systems that can't represent their own uncertainty — they hallucinate with full confidence, and we try to fix this with behavioral filters instead of architectural constraints. Second, every digital governance system — DAOs, voting platforms, democratic tools — weights participation by something that correlates poorly with informed judgment: token holdings, headcount, or identity.

Both problems share a root cause: our computing systems have no internal model of consciousness — no way to measure integration, coherence, or epistemic confidence as first-class values."

### What I Built (90s)
"Symthaea is a cognitive architecture in Rust that implements consciousness as a computational substrate. It runs a real-time loop integrating Hyperdimensional Computing, Integrated Information Theory, and Liquid Time-Constant neural networks. Its language generation system has epistemic gating — it physically cannot generate text beyond its measured confidence. This isn't a filter. It's a neuron that cannot fire when it doesn't know.

Mycelix is a decentralized governance system on Holochain — 16 domain clusters covering commons, health, governance, identity, finance, and more. Consciousness metrics from Symthaea gate civic participation: a 4-dimensional profile creates progressive tiers where engagement and coherence matter more than wealth or identity.

Together they form a universal trust fabric: Symthaea measures coherence and engagement, Mycelix uses those measurements to weight governance participation, and governance outcomes feed back into the system's dynamics."

### Why Open Source (30s)
"This is AGPL-3.0. If anyone modifies it and runs it as a service, they must share their modifications. If they won't, they need a commercial license. The commons is protected forever. The commercial hatch funds development. I built the legal framework this week — CLA, dual-license, the full structure."

### What I Need (30s)
"I've exhausted my savings. The code exists. 42,000 tests pass. 18 patents are drafted. 27 papers are in various stages. What I need is 12 months to finish the integration, deploy a pilot with a real community, publish the core contributions for peer review, and validate that the trust fabric works in practice with real communities."

### Close (30s)
"I'm not proposing to build something. I'm proposing to finish, validate, and deploy something that already exists and works. The Shuttleworth Foundation has always bet on individuals building open tools that change the structure of power. I believe this qualifies."
