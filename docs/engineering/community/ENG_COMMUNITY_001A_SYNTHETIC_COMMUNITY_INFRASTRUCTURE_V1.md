Reference/source subject for `ENG-COMMUNITY-001A` / issue #6156.

## Semantic ownership boundary

Mycelix remains the semantic owner of capital, stewardship, constituency, anti-capture, governance, service-covenant, return-envelope and transition-state semantics.

Symthaea consumes exact/current opaque references and projects their relationship to engineering assets, modules, operators, external authorities, physical configuration, lifecycle obligations, evidence and currentness.

Therefore:

```text
community economic ownership
!= legal title established
!= operator competence
!= operating licence
!= engineering safety
!= actuation authority
```

and:

```text
capital contribution
!= governance authority

operator authority
!= permanent ownership of commons assets

community governance decision
!= physical actuation capability
```

## Public reference campus

The v1 synthetic campus contains seven modules:

1. land/public realm — stewarded by a community/public-benefit steward;
2. thermal network — cooperative/stewarded operation;
3. water/wellness module — community operator;
4. greenhouse — community enterprise;
5. research facility — partnership operator;
6. compute/data-center module — private concession on a stewarded asset;
7. energy-project SPV — mixed capital with bounded claims and a qualified operator placeholder.

All identities are synthetic. The fixture intentionally demonstrates that community infrastructure may use mixed ownership and operating forms without collapsing the campus into one legal entity.

## Change/currentness law

Changes affect only the dimensions and dependants justified by exact impact analysis.

Examples:

```text
compute operator changes
-> compute concession/currentness changes
-> campus stewardship may remain unchanged
```

```text
material engineering configuration changes
-> engineering currentness/requalification may reopen
-> historical governance/capital evidence is not rewritten
```

```text
external licence/regulator requirement changes
-> applicability/currentness review
-> no automatic rewrite of community governance history
```

## Operator replacement law

A community or steward may have an upstream governance path for replacing an operator, but that does not itself grant replacement operating authority.

```text
community/steward decision ref
-> replacement proposal
-> operator qualification/licence checks
-> engineering/commissioning impacts
-> new operator generation
```

No step may be collapsed to `vote -> operate`.

## Reserve firewall

Required safety, resilience, lifecycle, maintenance, handback or decommissioning reserves remain distinct from distributable surplus.

```text
required reserve deficient
-> applicable distribution/transition blocker
-> ownership/stewardship state preserved independently
```

The exact financial arithmetic and distribution authority remain upstream Mycelix/external-owner concerns.

## Benefit vector

The reference architecture does not create a scalar `community_benefit_score`.

Potential public-benefit dimensions include tariff/service credits, district heat, community distributions, lifecycle reinvestment, research/education allocation, compute access, public wellness space, greenhouse/community-enterprise service, habitat/restoration funding, workforce/training participation and long-term economic ownership accrual.

Improvement in one dimension does not prove overall welfare or fairness.

## Corpus

The canonical JSON reference contains 24 hostile/reference cases. The required properties include:

- mixed ownership remains mixed;
- economic ownership without operator qualification creates no operating authority;
- operator competence does not create community ownership;
- capital does not mint governance authority;
- claim transfer does not transfer the commons asset;
- financial claim satisfaction does not automatically establish title or handback;
- service/reserve failures block only the claims authorized by upstream profiles;
- private concessions do not imply campus privatization;
- operator replacement remains qualification/licence gated;
- engineering and governance generations remain distinct;
- mandatory external constraints can block implementation;
- restricted dependencies remain structurally visible in public projections;
- synthetic fixtures cannot be represented as real deployments;
- multidimensional tradeoffs are preserved rather than averaged away;
- successful synthetic composition cannot promote legal, democratic, financial, engineering, licensing or operating authority.

## Claim ceiling

A later qualification PASS may establish only deterministic composition of the frozen synthetic ownership/steward/operator/capital/service/reserve/external-authority/currentness semantics and the declared hostile corpus.

- financial viability;
- social welfare or fair distribution;
- engineering safety;
- regulator approval;
- construction readiness;
