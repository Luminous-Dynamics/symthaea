# ENG-DESIGN-004A — Poetic Engineering Composition Contract

Status: source contract only. Parent: ENG-DESIGN-004 (#6096). Consumers: ENG-DESIGN-002 (#6090), ENG-DESIGN-003 (#6091), ENG-FORMAL-001 (#6089).

## Purpose

Define a bounded, evidence-aware representation for poetic engineering: design moves where authentic engineering, lifecycle, experiential, and stakeholder-authored symbolic roles reinforce one another.

This contract does not create a second aesthetic ontology and does not define beauty, meaning, taste, or cultural correctness.

## Core laws

```text
poetic intent != poetic success
semantic density != objective design quality
formal symmetry != beauty
symbolic coherence != universal meaning
engineering release != poetic success
model intent != as-built experience
poetic preference != safety/accessibility/maintenance authority
```

No scalar `poetry_score`, `beauty_score`, `meaning_score`, `aesthetic_score`, or universal ranking is permitted.

## PoeticMoveV1

A `PoeticMoveV1` is a composition reference over existing owners, not a new truth store. It should bind:

- stable feature/move identity;
- exact design/configuration subject;
- stakeholder-authored intent references;
- functional/engineering roles served;
- experiential/sensory roles served;
- symbolic/narrative references with source, audience, and context;
- owner references for structure/material/process/lifecycle facts;
- accessibility/human-factors references where relevant;
- maintenance/service/inspection consequences;
- protected qualities or relationships;
- explicitly non-protected incidental geometry;
- conflicts/tradeoffs;
- evidence and validation plan;
- change-impact triggers;
- claim ceiling.

## Composition rules

1. **No invented poetry.** If no poetic/experiential intent is declared, do not add one.
2. **Source symbolic claims.** Symbolic meaning requires an attributable stakeholder/context source.
3. **Hard constraints dominate.** Safety, accessibility, maintenance, inspection, code/authority, and physical constraints cannot be weakened by poetic preference.
4. **Prefer authentic multi-role moves.** One feature may legitimately serve technical and experiential roles, but role count is not a quality score.
5. **Protect relationships, not arbitrary shape.** Change impact should bind the actual relationship that matters, not every incidental coordinate.
6. **Keep model and realization separate.** A preserved CAD/BIM feature does not establish its as-built experiential effect.
7. **Keep technical and human validity separate.** Operational correctness may coexist with failed human-legibility or experiential validation.
8. **Retain disagreement.** Conflicting stakeholder interpretations remain attributed observations; do not average them into a universal meaning score.
9. **Treat ornament honestly.** Decoration without declared function/experience/meaning remains optional ornament and must not be promoted into a stronger claim.
10. **No authority promotion.** Poetic/aesthetic evidence cannot authorize fabrication, procurement, operation, or physical execution.

## Change impact

A design generation should trigger poetic review when it changes a declared carrier of intent, such as structural/module rhythm, aperture/daylight relationship, material or finish with sourced narrative, joint/fastener/service detail, circulation/threshold sequence, robot/interface motion timing, protected feature identity, maintenance/inspection route, acoustic/thermal/tactile behavior, or as-built obstruction/substitution.

Historical intent and validation remain append-only; stale evidence stays historical rather than disappearing.

## Evidence separation

Useful evidence may include engineering verification from canonical owners, protected-feature continuity checks, render/VR/mock-up review, physical material/prototype review, daylight/acoustic/spatial analysis, operator/maintainer review, stakeholder/community/cultural review where relevant, and as-built/post-occupancy/use observations.

None of these becomes universal beauty or meaning evidence.

## Formal-method boundary

ENG-FORMAL may prove bounded surrounding facts such as:

```text
protected dependency survives transformation
safety-class requirement cannot be weakened by poetic preference
maintenance access remains reachable after an enclosure transformation
export/import preserves protected feature identity or emits explicit loss
```

Formal proof must not be promoted into `beautiful`, `meaningful`, `moving`, or `culturally appropriate`.

## Reference corpus

The canonical corpus is `eng-design-004a-poetic-engineering-reference-v1` with 17 synthetic cases derived from raw design/process facts. An independent qualifier must derive the expected disposition without trusting `expected` as an oracle.

The corpus covers absent intent; captured human intent; authentic multi-role composition; unattributed symbolism; maintenance conflict; service + craft composition; protected-rhythm change impact; withdrawn symbolic source; accessibility conflict; material-narrative/provenance drift; as-built experiential drift; operationally-correct but human-illegible robot motion; protected-feature continuity; contested human interpretation; optional ornament; a positive engineering-release case with bounded human poetic validation; and an explicit authority-laundering attack proving poetic validation cannot mint physical execution authority.

## Claim ceiling

This contract may establish traceable composition between declared engineering roles and stakeholder-authored experiential/symbolic intents, plus bounded process dispositions for the exact synthetic cases.

It establishes no universal beauty, artistic superiority, cultural correctness, emotional response, architectural quality, accessibility compliance, safety, certification, regulatory approval, procurement/resource allocation, manufacturing readiness, or physical execution authority.
