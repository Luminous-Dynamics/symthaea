## 14. Explicit Nonclaims

Unless separately established by the appropriate canonical owner, this packet does not establish:

- external certification;
- real safety or regulatory sufficiency;
- production/process capability;
- service sufficiency;
- economics;
- procurement/resource allocation;
- physical actuation or execution authority.
