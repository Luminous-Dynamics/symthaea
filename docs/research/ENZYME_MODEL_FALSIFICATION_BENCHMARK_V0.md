# Enzyme model falsification benchmark V0

**Status:** deterministic synthetic harness implemented; real-assay replay, uncertainty calibration, and prospective value are not established.

## Run

From the repository root:

```sh
cargo test -p xtask --bin xtask enzyme_model_falsification
cargo run -p xtask -- enzyme-model-falsification --out reports/enzyme_model_falsification_v0.json
```

The command writes a JSON report to a local output path. The generated report is synthetic test output, not measured data, and should not be committed as experimental evidence.

## What V0 tests

### A. Known-truth parameter recovery

The fixture generates a deterministic Michaelis-Menten rate series, adds eight small prespecified residuals, and fits Vmax and Km with exhaustive grid search. The ground-truth values are Vmax=100 and Km=2, and both are included in the grid. The fixture gate requires relative error of at most 2% for each parameter.

This is one small correctness fixture, not a statistical estimate of typical recovery performance.

### B. Model-discrimination design toy

Two frozen hypotheses are compared:

- Michaelis-Menten: Vmax=100, Km=2.
- Substrate inhibition: Vmax=100, Km=2, Ki=20, with rate Vmax*S/(Km+S+S*S/Ki).

The design chooses among six synthetic substrate values by maximizing squared prediction separation normalized by an assumed measurement standard deviation of 2.0. It reports each candidate score and compares the selected score with the mean score under uniform random selection.

This does not fit the two models to observations, produce a calibrated hypothesis test, or recommend a real assay condition. The parameters and noise scale are fixed; physical feasibility and assay constraints are not modeled.

### C. Model-family selection on separable synthetic truths

V0 now also fits both model families by exhaustive grid search on two deterministic datasets: one generated by Michaelis-Menten and one generated by substrate inhibition. It compares their in-sample Bayesian Information Criterion (BIC) values. For this fixture only, the gate requires the generating family to be selected with an absolute BIC difference greater than 10.

This is a software falsification test for two deliberately separable cases. BIC is not a calibrated model-selection guarantee, two synthetic cases do not estimate general performance, and these in-sample tests cannot validate a real enzyme mechanism. V0 does not yet test near-equivalent models, varying noise regimes, misspecification, or held-out predictive performance.

### C. Local Km-sensitivity design proxy

A central finite difference estimates local sensitivity of the Michaelis-Menten rate to Km and selects the candidate with the largest sensitivity normalized by the assumed measurement standard deviation.

This is a local heuristic, not profile likelihood, a confidence interval, Fisher-information analysis, or proof of identifiability.

## Proposed benchmark scorecard

These are future research targets, not results claimed for V0. Expand scenarios and preregister the protocol before using them as release criteria.

| Gate | Proposed target | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Deterministic replay | 100% exact match for declared deterministic outputs; every injected mutation rejected | Independent replay and mutation suite |
| Parameter recovery | On identifiable synthetic cases, median absolute relative error <=5%, 95th percentile <=15% for each parameter | At least 1,000 preregistered scenarios spanning parameters, noise, designs, and seeds |
| Uncertainty calibration | Empirical coverage of nominal 95% intervals between 90% and 98% | Independent replicates; interval widths and failures also reported |
| Model discrimination | Balanced accuracy >=0.90 on prespecified separable cases | Multiple model pairs and matched noise levels |
| False certainty | <=5% of ambiguous or non-identifiable cases reported as decisive | Ground-truth ambiguity labels; count abstentions and unresolved results |
| Adaptive design efficiency | At least 25% fewer observations than uniform random to meet the same recovery gate, with no loss in coverage | At least 1,000 paired simulation trials and matched budgets; compare a simple sensitivity baseline too |
| External transport | Better preregistered primary metric than the strongest fair baseline, with uncertainty and denominators disclosed | Study/campaign holdouts and duplicate, context, and sequence-family leakage controls where applicable |
| Prospective utility | Positive cost-normalized decision improvement over random and established baselines in at least two independent campaigns | Predictions frozen before measurement, matched budgets, unsuccessful-result accounting, and qualified human review |

A benchmark passes only the gates it actually runs. A synthetic fixture cannot establish real-assay reproduction, external transport, or prospective utility.

## Paradigm shift: design measurements to resolve uncertainty

Report at least two distinct experiment-design objectives:

1. **Parameter identifiability:** select conditions that reduce uncertainty for a declared model and parameterization.
2. **Model discrimination:** select conditions at which plausible competing models make different, experimentally resolvable predictions.

These objectives can select different conditions. Do not hide the trade-off inside one opaque score. Later versions should report feasibility constraints, measurement budget, expected decision value, and what result would change the scientific conclusion.

V0 uses fixed hypotheses and a local sensitivity proxy only. Next, fit competing models to synthetic observations, include misspecification and non-identifiability, implement profile-likelihood or another justified uncertainty method, and measure whether adaptive designs improve recovery or model selection.

## Research basis

- [STRENDA official guidelines](https://www.beilstein-institut.de/en/projects/strenda/guidelines/): reporting guidance for enzyme identity, kinetic equations, assay conditions, replication, precision, and analysis. STRENDA does not define a universal data-quality score.
- [Optimal experiment design for practical parameter identifiability and model discrimination](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42176891/) (Mathematical Biosciences, September 2026): treats identifiability and model discrimination as related but distinct design objectives, demonstrated on ODE examples rather than this enzyme implementation.
- [EnzyArena preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.03.29.715152v2): condition-matched and independent mutagenesis evaluation motivate strong external-transfer tests. Its findings are preliminary and should be independently reproduced.
- [EnzymeML V2 specification](https://github.com/EnzymeML/enzymeml-specifications/blob/main/specifications/v2.md): candidate interoperable representation for experimental data and kinetic models; Symthaea's provenance layer should complement, not recreate, that model.

## Scope guard

A JSON report, structural checker, fitted curve, or retrospective score is not proof that a prediction is true, a parameter is identifiable, or a future experiment will improve a decision. Keep these claims separated in all reports.
