# PLASMA-FAILURE-COMPOSITION-001 — full failure-mode distribution evidence

**Date:** 2026-11-14

**Stack:** #12028 → #12051 → #12070 → #12074 → this branch

## Executive finding

The prior transfer fixture treated `failure_mode_fraction` like another continuous scalar endpoint. That is not enough to qualify how a joint fails.

Failure modes form a structured category distribution. A percentage such as "cohesive failure" can remain unchanged while adhesive failure rises and fiber tear falls. A single scalar can conceal the rest of the joint's failure phenotype.

The paradigm shift is from **one failure-mode fraction** to a **complete, specimen-level failure composition**, linked to observers, category ontology, independent lots and a preregistered multivariate result.

This remains separate from:
- joint strength (D3163/D5868);
- adhesive constitutive behavior (D5656);
- descriptive failure-mode classification (D5573);
- service-life / competing-failure time (survival evidence);
- product-specific acceptability and disposition.

## Standards boundary

ASTM D5573-99R19 is an active practice for classifying, identifying and characterizing failure modes in adhesively bonded FRP joints. Its development scope is glass-fiber / thermoset-polyester sheet-molding compound (SMC). ASTM states that classification is based on visual observation of the failure surface without a microscope or other magnification, and that the practice does **not** address acceptability of any specific failure mode. It may describe failure modes generated by test methods such as D3163 and D5868.

Official source: https://store.astm.org/d5573-99r19.html

The S0 contract therefore:
- reuses the exact D5573 method-to-claim rule from #12072;
- requires the D5573-99R19 / SMC FRP / failure-surface classification scope;
- records image artifacts for traceability but requires the observation mode to remain `direct_unaided_visual`;
- rejects classification-as-acceptance claims;
- does not infer that cohesive, fiber-tear or any other category is good/bad without an independent product-specific acceptance policy.

## S0 implementation

Rust module: `xtask/src/plasma_failure_composition.rs`  
Fixture: `data/research/plasma_failure_composition_fixture_v0_1.json`  
Command:

```bash
cargo run -p xtask -- plasma-failure-composition-audit
```

The receipt binds:
- category-ontology ID/version and exact ordered category IDs;
- one complete vector for each specimen;
- specimen, independent manufacturing cluster, image digest, observer identities and blinding state;
- direct unaided visual observation mode for D5573;
- D5573 classification receipt, exact method/edition/evidence kind/material/geometry/endpoint/claim and specimen manifest;
- a separate fraction-measurement protocol and observer reliability receipt;
- measurement basis (`visual_area_fraction` or `specimen_count_fraction`);
- explicit missing/unclassifiable specimens and unresolved disagreement;
- baseline/candidate cohort and independent-unit semantics;
- preregistered zero-handling and pseudocount policy;
- frozen plan, per-category equivalence margins, joint compositional-distance margin, alpha/confidence and method reference;
- full vector-manifest digest and analysis result;
- per-category effect intervals and joint-distance interval.

The audit does not calculate Aitchison distance, Dirichlet-multinomial/logistic-normal models, cluster bootstrap, or compositional hypothesis tests. It validates that an externally qualified analysis receipt reports the full category vector and is tied to the frozen plan and manifest.

### Measurement basis is explicit

- Under `visual_area_fraction`, each specimen contains a continuous allocation of the observed failure surface over the declared category set.
- Under `specimen_count_fraction`, each specimen must be one-hot: exactly one category has fraction 1 and all others 0. A fractional surface mixture cannot be relabeled as a count-based category.

The two bases cannot be pooled in a single comparison. The fraction protocol is separate from D5573 classification: D5573 provides the descriptive category system, while a separately documented procedure defines how fractional area or specimen counts are recorded.

Each specimen vector must sum to one within the receipt's declared tolerance. The S0 tolerance cap of 0.05 is a software guardrail for this prototype, not an ASTM requirement or laboratory standard.

### Analysis result is distinct from study validity

A structurally valid study can yield:
- `PASS / COMPOSITIONAL_EQUIVALENCE_ESTABLISHED`, when every category interval fits its preregistered margin and the joint-distance upper interval fits its margin; or
- `PASS / COMPOSITIONAL_EQUIVALENCE_NOT_ESTABLISHED`, when the design/evidence chain is valid but the full composition fails the equivalence criterion.

This distinction prevents a valid study design from being misrepresented as a positive material result.

## Research direction and SOTA

The key research problem is not maximizing one failure-mode percentage. It is preserving the full distribution and validating how it varies across independent production groups.

The future physical programme should compare:
1. one scalar mode fraction;
2. mode-count histogram without lot-level grouping;
3. full specimen-level vector without observer reliability;
4. full vector with observer reliability, cluster-aware design, preregistration and independent analysis.

Proposed metrics:
- full-vector manifest completeness;
- closure error by specimen;
- observer agreement and unresolved-disagreement rate;
- independent-lot coverage;
- per-category effect uncertainty;
- multivariate compositional distance and uncertainty;
- false equivalence and over-abstention;
- category-specific shifts masked by scalar summaries;
- sensitivity to near-zero/structural-zero handling;
- transfer performance on held-out material lots/sites.

Prospective software-contract targets:
- zero PASS for incomplete or silently renormalized vectors;
- zero count-based pseudo-replication accepted as independent lots;
- 100% detection of method/ontology/specimen/cohort mismatches;
- 100% detection of post-outcome category, zero-policy or margin changes;
- zero scalar summaries accepted in place of the full vector;
- deterministic replay of the frozen vector contract.

These are regression targets, not measurements of physical classification accuracy, POD, reliability or product safety.

## Strategic implication

This creates a typed joint phenotype rather than a generic "bond quality score":

surface state → adhesive constitutive response → joint strength/fracture response → full failure-mode distribution → aging/competing failure → product-specific acceptance policy.

Those transitions are evidence dependencies, not automatic implications. In particular:
- low contact angle cannot override weak lap-shear evidence;
- D5656 constitutive response cannot stand in for substrate adhesion;
- D5573 classification cannot establish acceptability;
- a scalar failure-mode fraction cannot replace the full distribution.
