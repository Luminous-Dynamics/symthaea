# VART-WORLD-CREATIVE-001 — Stop/Go Gates

## STOP conditions

Stop and create a new source-qualified lineage if any of the following occurs:

- a production mechanism change is needed after confirmatory source freeze;
- `RevisionHypothesis` can be written or modified after the corresponding world mutation;
- baseline policies receive a different admissible candidate surface without preregistered justification;
- random-valid can select a physically inadmissible candidate;
- `CreativeTrial` can close with a missing revisit observation or mismatched world version;
- counterfactual, replay, dream, or historical evidence can be substituted for committed observation;
- missing/dropped evidence is silently treated as continuity;
- an aggregate world/creative quality scalar enters the qualified result surface;
- pilot observations are reused as confirmatory observations;
- a fixture, seed, threshold, stopping rule, policy implementation, or outcome definition changes after confirmatory outcomes are inspected;
- the independent verifier disagrees with runtime closure.

## GO to confirmatory execution only when

- exact source HEAD/TREE is remotely reachable and independently verified;
- noncanonical pilot closes all required evidence chains;
- pilot evidence root is cryptographically and operationally separate from confirmatory evidence;
- fixture corpus and digests are frozen;
- seeds are frozen;
- policy and ablation implementations are frozen;
- candidate/revision budgets and stopping rules are frozen;
- all measured channels and missing-data semantics are frozen;
- thresholds are frozen prospectively;
- independent verifier identity is frozen;
- `confirmatory_authorized=true` is explicitly recorded.

## GO to bounded claim only when

- all preregistered confirmatory cells complete or are accounted for under the frozen missing/abort policy;
- baseline and ablation comparisons are independently reconstructed;
- all protected physical/provenance invariants pass;
- no selective trial omission is detected;
- the independent verifier seals the final evidence capsule;
- `claim_authorized=true` is explicitly recorded after verification.

Maximum admitted claim:

`EvidenceBoundExperienceConditionedWorldImprovementQualified`
