# GEOM-002C — Geometric/Causal Profile Bridge

## Status

Implementation tranche for #2970, stacked on GEOM-002B exact head `571b9ef890bbc80dc58df6dca6536541f1ab3b87`.

This tranche is not evidence that gravity and consciousness are physically related. It is a reporting bridge between two already-independent measurement families.

## Purpose

GEOM-001 measures trajectories in probability space with Fisher-Rao geometry.

GEOM-002A/002B measure multiscale causal structure and sensitivity to declared coarse-grainings.

GEOM-002C places those measurements in one typed report so later lesion/rescue experiments can ask whether they change together.

## Non-composition rule

The bridge MUST NOT create:

- a combined consciousness score;
- a gravity/consciousness similarity score;
- a weighted average of geometric and causal metrics;
- a rank that silently privileges one theory;
- a Boolean saying that geometry and causal emergence jointly prove consciousness.

Geometry and causality remain separate fields.

## Joint profile

A joint profile contains:

### Geometry

- Fisher-Rao path length;
- endpoint displacement;
- geodesic efficiency;
- excess path length;
- step-length statistics.

### Causal profile

- finest-scale effective information;
- peak-scale effective information;
- peak scale index;
- advantage versus finest scale;
- preregistered criterion result.

### Sensitivity, when available

- number of preregistered partition variants;
- peak-scale stability;
- criterion-pass fraction;
- maximum causal-advantage envelope width.

No arithmetic combines these families.

## Intervention contrasts

GEOM-003 will compare a reference condition to lesions, shams and rescues.

GEOM-002C therefore exposes deltas separately:

`Δ geometry = geometry(condition) - geometry(reference)`

and

`Δ causal = causal(condition) - causal(reference)`.

Sensitivity deltas are likewise separate.

The bridge intentionally does not output a concordance coefficient in this tranche. Correlation or concordance analysis across repeated interventions requires its own preregistration, sample-size assumptions and null model.

## Fail-closed comparability

A contrast is rejected when:

1. probability-state dimensionality differs between conditions;
2. the causal state-count profile differs between conditions;
3. sensitivity information is present for only one condition.

A joint profile rejects sensitivity envelopes whose scale count or state-count profile does not match the causal sweep being summarized.

## Claim boundary

Allowed claim:

> Under the declared measurements, condition B changed Fisher-Rao trajectory geometry by X and multiscale causal quantities by Y relative to condition A.

Not allowed:

> Because both changed, consciousness curved action space.

Not allowed:

> Because both changed, cognition and gravity share a physical mechanism.

Not allowed:

> A combined score proves consciousness.

## Downstream gate

GEOM-003 may use this bridge for controlled lesion/sham/rescue experiments only after the predecessor measurement implementations are qualified. GEOM-004 gravity-analogue work remains blocked until intervention results establish stable, reproducible observables that survive null and sensitivity controls.
