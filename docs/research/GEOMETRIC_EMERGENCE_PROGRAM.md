# GEOM — Geometric Emergence Research Program

Status: experimental research program.  This document defines measurement and claim boundaries; it does not assert that consciousness and gravity share a physical mechanism.

## Core hypothesis

Test the structural proposition

`local interactions -> multiscale integration -> effective geometry -> constrained macroscopic dynamics`

across independently defined systems.  A repeated mathematical pattern is evidence for a reusable organizational principle, not evidence of physical identity.

## GEOM-001 — theory-neutral observatory

The first stage measures trajectories through normalized probability space using Fisher-Rao geometry.  It records:

- Fisher-Rao path length;
- endpoint displacement;
- geodesic efficiency = endpoint displacement / path length;
- excess path length;
- mean, variance, and maximum step length;
- Fisher-Rao response to controlled perturbations.

All probability inputs are validated and normalized before measurement.  Invalid, non-finite, negative, zero-mass, or dimensionally inconsistent inputs fail closed.

### Null and positive controls

Required baseline families include:

1. stationary trajectories — zero path length;
2. monotonic binary-simplex trajectories — approximately unit geodesic efficiency;
3. matched-endpoint detours — longer path and lower efficiency than the direct trajectory;
4. self perturbations — zero response;
5. changed distributions — positive response.

Passing these controls establishes only that the observatory measures its declared geometry.

## Terminology boundary

`D_KL(p(a,b) || p(a)p(b))` is mutual information `I(A;B)`.  It is useful as an integration/dependence statistic, but it is not IIT Phi and is not a curvature scalar.  The canonical implementation name is therefore `mutual_information_integration`.  The old `phi_information_geometric` symbol is retained only as a compatibility wrapper.

No scalar in GEOM-001 is a consciousness score.

## GEOM-002 — multiscale causal geometry

Issue #2964.  Sweep declared coarse-grainings and report effective information, determinism, degeneracy, and macro causal advantage at each scale.  Discretization sensitivity and shuffled nulls are mandatory.  `CE > 0` must not be translated directly into `consciousness emerges` or metaphysical downward causation.

## GEOM-003 — lesion and rescue

Issue #2965.  Compare intact, partitioned, shuffled-connectivity, feed-forward, and selectively lesioned architectures under matched compute/activity conditions.  Freeze observables and decision rules before inspecting intervention outcomes.  Exact rescue is preferred where the architecture permits it.

A particularly strong result would be a geometric change that reliably precedes an overt performance decline.  That would support the geometry as an order parameter for organization, not automatically as a consciousness marker.

## GEOM-004 — gravity analogue

Issue #2966.  This stage remains blocked until the earlier stages produce stable, reproducible observables.  Compare independently derived cognitive-state geometry and emergent-spacetime/toy-gravity systems using preregistered invariants such as:

- geodesic structure;
- curvature proxies;
- effective dimensionality;
- information bottlenecks;
- coarse-graining flow;
- boundary/volume scaling;
- phase-transition signatures.

Similarity is to be reported as mathematical analogy unless an independent physical derivation establishes a bridge.

## Claim ladder

Allowed claims must remain at the lowest supported rung:

1. measurement implementation is correct on declared controls;
2. a geometric observable distinguishes preregistered conditions;
3. the observable generalizes to held-out tasks/seeds/systems;
4. the observable predicts later dynamics or intervention response;
5. independently defined physical and cognitive models share specified invariants;
6. a physical bridge is derived and independently tested.

Evidence for a lower rung never licenses a higher rung automatically.

## Explicitly blocked claims

Until independently established, GEOM must not claim that:

- consciousness is gravity;
- consciousness creates spacetime curvature;
- gravity causes consciousness;
- mutual information is IIT Phi;
- causal emergence proves downward causation;
- an HDC similarity or binding operation is physical quantum entanglement;
- a shared mathematical invariant demonstrates shared ontology.

The desired outcome is a research instrument that can falsify the motivating analogy as cleanly as it can support a narrower structural hypothesis.
