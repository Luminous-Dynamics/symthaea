## Evidence rule

Every backend observation must declare its execution boundary and resource accounting boundary. Simulation is not hardware evidence. Device-level energy is not whole-system energy. Exotic substrates must beat matched conventional controls on frozen workloads before integration is considered.

## Integration rule

Research backends remain shadow-only until their contracts and experiments qualify independently. They do not alter the active HDC/LTC scientific subject. A future NEUROARCH adapter is blocked on qualification of the neutral topology contract.
