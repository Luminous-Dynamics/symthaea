# PARADOX-000 — Structured Inconsistency Preregistration

Authority: **MeasurementOnly**

Tracks: #3165, #3166

## Research question

Does persistent structured inconsistency selectively recruit metacognitive processing in Symthaea beyond what is explained by task difficulty, external surprise, or computational load?

This document freezes the conceptual distinctions, primary hypotheses, and falsifiers before PARADOX experimental execution.

## Non-equivalences

The following are explicitly not treated as synonyms:

- prediction error != internal disagreement;
- disagreement != uncertainty;
- uncertainty != poor evidence quality;
- cognitive conflict != consciousness;
- workspace access != consciousness;
- metacognitive recruitment != consciousness;
- integration coherence != awareness;
- geometric trajectory structure != consciousness;
- IIT Phi != the generic integration/transition metrics used elsewhere in Symthaea;
- apparent contradiction in an agent's evidence != proof that reality is logically inconsistent.

## Frozen taxonomy

### PredictionError

A model predicts one observation and receives a materially different observation.

### Ambiguity

Multiple hypotheses remain compatible with the available evidence.

### ActionConflict

Two or more processes recommend mutually incompatible actions under the same decision context.

### EvidenceContradiction

Qualified evidence supports a proposition and qualified evidence supports its negation or an explicitly incompatible proposition.

### Underdetermination

The admitted evidence cannot distinguish between candidate hypotheses or actions.

### SelfReferentialConflict

A prediction or representation about the agent changes the state whose truth the prediction or representation concerns.

### OntologyFailure

The current representational vocabulary cannot jointly account for the admitted observations without introducing a latent context, splitting a category, or otherwise revising the model class.

### FormalParadox

An explicitly constructed logical or self-referential paradox. Formal paradox is a separate experimental class and must not be inferred merely from cognitive conflict.

## Primary hypothesis

> Persistent structured inconsistency selectively recruits metacognitive processing beyond matched task difficulty, surprise, and computational load; transient conflict alone need not do so.

## Secondary hypothesis

> Successful metacognitive processing can sometimes convert persistent apparent contradiction into representational expansion, after which conflict and metacognitive recruitment relax.

Neither hypothesis is assumed by the implementation.

## Measurement separation

PARADOX-001 must preserve distinct observables for:

- external surprise;
- internal disagreement;
- uncertainty;
- persistence;
- self-referential relevance;
- evidence polarity;
- integration coherence;
- conflict load;
- candidate metacognitive recruitment;
- resolution state.

In particular, it must be possible to represent both:

```text
high external surprise + low internal disagreement
```

and:

```text
low external surprise + high internal disagreement
```

without either state being projected onto the other.

## Coherence / metacognition separation

One current enhanced-consciousness path derives a self-awareness quantity from cross-theory agreement. PARADOX treats cross-theory agreement as a candidate **coherence** measurement rather than defining awareness by it.

The program therefore permits this empirical pattern:

```text
internal disagreement increases
integration coherence decreases
metacognitive recruitment increases
```

without asserting that the pattern must occur.

## Evidence polarity

The first observatory preserves positive and negative support independently using a four-state relation:

- `Neither`
- `SupportsPropositionOnly`
- `SupportsNegationOnly`
- `SupportsBoth`

`SupportsBoth` means only that the admitted inputs support incompatible propositions. It is not an ontological claim that reality itself contains a contradiction.

## Initial matched-condition matrix

Later experimental tranches should compare at least:

1. low surprise / low disagreement;
2. high surprise / low disagreement;
3. low surprise / high disagreement;
4. transient disagreement;
5. persistent resolvable contradiction;
6. persistent irreducible contradiction;
7. self-referential conflict;
8. ontology failure with a hidden contextual variable.

Where practical, controls should match task length, evidence volume, resource budget, and baseline difficulty.

## Primary falsifiers

The primary hypothesis is weakened or falsified if matched experiments show that:

1. metacognitive recruitment tracks surprise or task difficulty but not structured inconsistency;
2. transient and persistent conflict recruit indistinguishable dynamics;
3. metacognitive or self-model lesions change contradiction handling only through generic performance loss;
4. apparent effects disappear when evidence polarity and provenance are preserved;
5. ontology-revision outcomes are no better than arbitrary hypothesis expansion;
6. geometric effects are fully explained by ordinary task difficulty or path length;
7. conclusions depend on post-hoc thresholds, post-hoc condition definitions, or hand-selected scenarios.

## Claim boundary

PARADOX measurements may support claims about relationships among conflict, uncertainty, metacognition, self-modeling, workspace dynamics, representational revision, and task performance.

They do not by themselves establish consciousness, sentience, phenomenal experience, suffering, moral patienthood, AGI, IIT Phi, or a physical identity/relationship between consciousness and logical paradox.
