## Abstract pattern smoke results

Configuration: bipolar MAP-style patterns, 16,384 coordinates, 64 candidate prototypes, 16 trials per condition, fixed seed. Estimated prototype payload is 1,048,576 bytes (64 × 16,384 × 1 byte), excluding allocation metadata.

| Check | Observed result | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Binding/unbinding similarity | 1.000000 | The bipolar algebraic round-trip behaved exactly as expected. |
| Bundling ablation similarity | -0.009644 | A simple superposition did not preserve the tested filler when incorrectly unbound. |
| Antipattern cancellation absolute similarity | 0.006714 | Bundling a pattern with its complement produced a near-orthogonal result in this trial. |
| Sequence-order similarity | -0.007324 | The two order encodings remained distinct in this trial. |
| Basis equivariance | true | The tested coordinate permutation preserved the binding relation exactly. |
| Cached runner elapsed time | 1,513 ms | Exploratory run only; not a controlled latency benchmark. |

Nearest-prototype top-1 retrieval and the projected-softmax cleanup baseline both scored 16/16 on each corruption level from 5% through 45%, then 0/16 at 50%. The 16/16 Wilson 95% interval was approximately [0.806, 1.000], and the 0/16 interval [0.000, 0.194]. This only brackets the failure region coarsely. The stored-library and bundled-association recall tests also scored 16/16 through 49 associations at 16,384 dimensions with 64 candidates. That configuration is too easy to distinguish meaningful capacity between methods. It motivated the high-capacity stress mode (4,096 dimensions, 256 candidates, capacities through 255) and the byte-matched HRR comparison, which need current-head execution.

## Cell Foundry multiscale results

The earlier run produced 240 condition records: four deterministic seeds, radial and anterior/posterior target families, two removal severities plus full Vmem scramble, five gap-junction permeability levels, and homing on/off. The raw trajectories are in the artifact's `levin-multiscale-results.json`. In the original output, each scramble seed appeared as a distinct grouping label; the current example now normalizes the group label so the four seeds aggregate correctly in its summary.

Weighted mean final discrepancy by morphology family and homing condition, averaging the three perturbation classes with four seed observations each:

| Target family | Positional homing | Permeability 0.0 | Permeability 1.0 | Direction |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Axis | Off | 0.3562 | 0.3520 | Small improvement |
| Axis | On | 0.2754 | 0.2867 | Worse |
| Radial | Off | 0.2590 | 0.2425 | Improvement |
| Radial | On | 0.1992 | 0.1956 | Small improvement |

Selected per-perturbation observations show why the family-wide mean is not a sufficient result:

- Axis removal `[0.35, 2.00)`, homing off: discrepancy changed from 0.2832 at permeability 0.0 to 0.2770 at 1.0.
- The same axis removal, homing on: discrepancy changed from 0.1844 to 0.2037 (worse).
- Complete axis Vmem scramble, homing on: discrepancy changed from 0.1357 to 0.1508 (worse).
- Radial removal `[1.10, 2.00)`, homing off: discrepancy improved from 0.1818 to 0.1581 as permeability rose from 0.0 to 1.0.

Positional homing lowered discrepancy in the tested conditions, whereas stronger gap-junction coupling had mixed effects and could hurt axis-specific recovery. That is a useful negative result: the current model does **not** establish that more coupling is always better. It suggests a new experiment—adaptive, target-error-conditioned coupling—rather than hard-coding a universal monotonic relationship.

## Conclusions that are not justified yet

- These synthetic simulations do not validate the model against living tissue or establish basal cognition.
- Sixteen or four trial-level observations are not enough to assert statistically robust superiority.
- The simple cleanup baseline is Hopfield-inspired, not a faithful replication of a specific modern Hopfield network.
- Perfect retrieval at low load is not a state-of-the-art result. The meaningful tests are capacity at matched byte budget, composition generalization, uncertainty intervals, runtime/memory trade-offs, and robust functional recovery across unseen structures.
- The preliminary log is from an earlier checkout, not the latest branch head. Later HRR, matched-byte, stress-mode, and aggregation changes need their own exact-head artifact and passing gates.

## Next decision

Keep the mixed coupling result. The branch now includes two adaptive-coupling conditions: a deliberately simple discrepancy-trend heuristic and a paired counterfactual controller that evaluates lower/current/higher permeability from cloned states using matched RNG state. The current-head experiment will compare both against the mean of fixed permeability settings 0.0, 0.5, and 1.0 on the same seed/target/damage/homing condition. On the abstract side, use the current high-capacity MAP/HRR stress runner, then expand to at least 100 trials per capacity point for credible K95 estimates; compare both equal dimensions and equal payload bytes.
