# VART-WORLD-CREATIVE-001 — Archive Capsule v1

Status: post-confirmatory archival contract. This document does not alter VART-001, authorize a broader claim, or permit tuning against VART-001 evidence.

## Purpose

VART-001 is a completed benchmark-family experiment. Its scientific value depends on preserving the exact subject, instrument, frozen contracts, evidence root, analysis lineage, and bounded claim packet that produced the result.

The archive capsule exists to make VART-001 reproducible while preventing it from becoming a development benchmark.

## Immutable capsule contents

A qualified capsule MUST bind raw-byte SHA-256 digests for:

- Freeze v3 and its external anchor record;
- complete frozen trial inventory and deterministic run schedule;
- subject source closure receipt and exact subject HEAD/TREE;
- pre-execution instrument qualification/source-closure receipt and exact instrument HEAD/TREE;
- analysis/metric/calibration/missingness/multiplicity contracts;
- sealed 64-trial campaign receipt and evidence-closure digest;
- original first-unblinding pre-anchor and analysis receipt;
- post-unblinding correction/audit receipts, without deleting or replacing the original analysis receipt;
- final bounded claim packet and claim-audit result;
- every superseded freeze/analysis lineage with explicit predecessor links;
- toolchain/lock/environment identity needed for fresh-checkout reproduction.

The capsule manifest itself is content-addressed and externally anchored after assembly.

## Permanent anti-tuning rule

After archive qualification, VART-001 is **evaluation-history only**.

The following are prohibited as development feedback for later Symthaea versions:

- optimizing architecture or weights against VART-001 trial outcomes;
- selecting thresholds, candidate budgets, prompts, retrieval rules, or policy parameters because they improve VART-001;
- using VART-001 hidden fixtures or exact seeds as regression targets;
- repeatedly rerunning modified Symthaea versions on the frozen confirmatory worlds and choosing the best result;
- promoting exploratory post-hoc findings into the VART-001 confirmatory claim family.

Allowed uses include reproducibility checks, verifier regression tests using synthetic fixtures, methodological audits, and historical comparison clearly labeled non-confirmatory.

## Claim ceiling

The strongest allowed VART-001 claim remains benchmark-family bounded. Archive qualification does not broaden it to general creativity, general intelligence, consciousness, universal causal learning, or universal necessity of any architecture component.
