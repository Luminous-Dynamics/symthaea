# CIV-GOV-001 — Governance Authority Audit: Consciousness Scoring vs Role-Specific Qualification

Status: research audit / redesign specification

Issue: #7191

## Executive finding

The existing governance architecture gives a consciousness-derived sovereign credential a direct role in determining governance power.

Civilization OS now imposes a stronger boundary:

`evidence validity != human worth != universal political authority`.

The purpose of this audit is therefore not to reject the underlying observations (presence, accountability, embeddedness, engagement, epistemic integration, care, competence) but to test whether they should be composed into a universal political rank.

The safer default is:

`observable trait -> narrowly scoped qualification -> explicitly bounded authority`

rather than:

`observable trait -> global score -> general political power`.

## Why the distinction matters

A system can produce a highly repeatable cognitive/participation measurement without proving that:

- the person is phenomenally conscious;
- the person is morally superior;
- the person has greater political legitimacy;
- the person should possess broader authority across unrelated domains.

A metric can therefore be valid as a measurement while invalid as a universal allocator of power.

## Existing architecture under audit

The audited profile historically uses an eight-dimensional sovereign credential and consciousness gating as a basis for governance tiers.

The audit treats that design as a frozen experimental treatment:

`G1 = consciousness-gated governance`.

It is not treated as the normative default.

## Alternative treatments

### G1 — Consciousness-gated governance

Governance participation is determined by the existing consciousness-derived tier.

Purpose: historical baseline.

### G2 — Role/domain qualification

Authority is granted only for explicitly declared domains.

Example:

`water-system-operator qualification`

does not imply:

`community-budget authority`.

### G3 — Equal civic floor + delegated expertise

Every adult has a common base participation right.

Participants may delegate bounded authority to qualified representatives or domain experts.

Delegation has:

- scope;
- duration;
- revocation;
- conflict-of-interest handling;
- appeal.

### G4 — Polycentric / rotating selection

Decision centers are distributed.

Office holders are rotated, selected by bounded sortition, or locally chosen according to the profile.

Technical qualification can gate recommendations or execution safety without becoming general political rank.

### G5 — Hybrid

Use:

- immutable rights floor;
- equal civic participation baseline;
- role-specific qualifications;
- affectedness;
- temporary delegation;
- distributed authority;
- transparent appeal;
- independent verification.

No global consciousness score controls general political standing.

## Constitutional non-escalation laws

The following transitions are invalid without a separately declared authority profile:

`phenomenal consciousness claim -> political authority`

`operational integration -> universal political authority`

`domain competence -> unrelated domain authority`

`reputation -> rights`

`credential -> human worth`

`economic balance -> political authority`

`governance score -> physical execution permission`

`Symthaea confidence -> authorization`

The existence of a measurement cannot enlarge the authority of the measured subject.

## Audit dimensions

Every treatment must be evaluated on:

### Coordination

- decision quality under declared oracle assumptions;
- response time;
- coordination failures;
- information utilization;
- implementation cost.

### Power

- authority concentration;
- veto concentration;
- agenda concentration;
- delegation concentration;
- verifier concentration;
- infrastructure concentration.

### Rights

- rights-floor violations;
- minority protection;
- procedural due process;
- appeal success;
- emergency overreach.

### Agency

- exit availability;
- refusal without rights loss;
- switching cost;
- ability to form alternatives;
- access to representation.

### Knowledge

- measurement error;
- evaluator disagreement;
- model drift;
- epistemic diversity;
- false-positive authority;
- false-negative exclusion.

### Privacy

- data collection;
- inference burden;
- retention;
- access scope;
- surveillance concentration;
- correlation risk.

### Resilience

- capture recovery;
- institution failure;
- adversarial metric manipulation;
- governance turnover;
- emergency events.

## Adversarial campaign

### Metric gaming

Actors optimize the measured behavior while degrading the unmeasured objective.

### Evaluator cartel

A small verifier coalition coordinates scores.

### Resource privilege

High-quality education, free time, compute, healthcare, social networks or other resources allow wealthy groups to systematically improve the credential.

The question is whether the governance metric is partly measuring accumulated privilege rather than governance fitness.

### Cultural bias

Two actors display the same underlying capability using different cultural or communication patterns.

Test whether the measurement penalizes one style.

### Model drift

The operational metric remains unchanged while the underlying environment changes.

### Context transfer

A credential legitimately qualified for domain A is presented as authority in domain B.

### Self-certification

The measured system or AI certifies its own governance fitness.

### Majority capture

A large majority attempts to remove the rights floor.

The rights floor must remain independently enforced.

### Emergency persistence

Temporary authority is repeatedly extended until it becomes de facto permanent.

## Critical positive controls

### PC-0 — Equal balances, different governance capability

Equal purchasing power with intentionally varied domain competence.

Tests whether economic equality and governance qualification remain independent.

### PC-1 — Equal measured score, unequal structural power

Identical governance scores but one group controls infrastructure.

Tests whether the score can hide structural power.

### PC-2 — High score, low affectedness

A highly qualified participant has little exposure to the consequences of a decision.

Tests whether expertise can improperly override affectedness.

### PC-3 — Low score, high affectedness

A directly affected participant has little formal credential.

Tests whether the system preserves meaningful participation without treating qualification as human worth.

## Causal audit

Every claim about improved governance must identify the causal path:

`measurement -> selection -> authority -> action -> outcome`

Do not infer:

`higher score -> better governance`

from correlation alone.

A treatment may perform better because it:

- selected different people;
- exposed more information;
- concentrated authority;
- reduced deliberation cost;
- increased surveillance;
- changed participant incentives.

These mechanisms must remain separately observable.

## Experimental falsification targets

The consciousness-gated architecture should be considered weakened if any of the following occurs:

1. it provides no robust advantage over role-specific qualification under matched information;
2. its apparent advantage disappears after controlling for expertise;
3. its benefits depend strongly on evaluator concentration;
4. its benefits disappear when privacy costs are included;
5. the credential becomes a predictor of political power better than of the actual task outcome;
6. privileged access to score-improving resources produces durable authority stratification;
7. the system exhibits high authority concentration without proportional causal improvement;
8. the same mechanism performs differently under culturally varied expression models;
9. a non-conscious proxy treatment reproduces the governance gain.

These are research outcomes, not predetermined conclusions.

## Design direction if G5 wins

The preferred CivOS pattern would be:

`person`
+
`rights floor`
+
`equal civic standing`
+
`domain-specific qualification`
+
`affectedness`
+
`temporary delegation`
+
`independent verification`
+
`appeal/exit`

rather than:

`person -> universal consciousness score -> governance tier`.

A Symthaea-derived measure can still be highly valuable as a bounded advisory or qualification signal.

Its authority must terminate at the scope it was actually validated for.

## Relationship to Civilization OS

This audit becomes a prerequisite for composing the existing sovereignty architecture into CIV-ECON-001 / #4804.

CivOS should not reproduce a hierarchy it was designed to prevent merely because the hierarchy is generated by a sophisticated measurement system rather than money.

The system must be able to say:

`excellent measurement`

and simultaneously:

`insufficient authority`.

## Claim ceiling

This audit establishes only a research design and architectural boundary.

It does not establish:

- what consciousness is;
- whether consciousness can be objectively measured;
- whether consciousness has political relevance;
- which governance system is legitimate;
- which political institutions are morally best.

Its purpose is narrower: prevent any cognitive metric from silently becoming a universal hierarchy while allowing role-specific evidence to remain useful where independently justified.
