# PERCEPT-COR-001 — Evidence audit and falsifiable protocol for “Code of Reality”

**Date:** 2026-10-29  
**Tracking issue:** https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symthaea/issues/8970  
**Status:** literature/source audit and protocol proposal; no participant data analyzed and no new experiment performed.

## Executive assessment

The “Code of Reality” claim associated with Danny Goler is a report of striking subjective visual phenomenology: under a DMT-induced altered state, some observers viewing a diffracted laser pattern describe coherent geometric structures and glyph-like characters, sometimes interpreted as an external code.

The strongest careful conclusion today is:

- **A real experience can occur without the proposed interpretation being true.** The subjective report is itself a legitimate target of research.
- **The published pilot is not enough to establish a shared external symbol field.** The IPI Letters record is presented as a News and Views item. The public abstract describes recurring reports but does not provide a blinded cross-observer correspondence analysis, participant-level dataset, preregistered endpoint, independently checkable null denominator, or independent confirmatory replication.
- **A perceptual/neural explanation is plausible but not complete.** DMT has measurable effects on visual processing. A 2024 within-subject MRI study reports increased population receptive-field sizes in peripheral V1 under the active DMT condition, associated with visual effects. This does not itself explain the specific glyph reports, but it provides a grounded mechanistic direction.
- **The “code proves reality is a simulation” interpretation is a further inference.** It does not follow from visual vividness, repeated motifs, or subjective conviction.

## Decompose the claim before testing

### H1 — Phenomenology
A subset of observers report coherent geometric or glyph-like imagery under the claimed condition.

Appropriate evidence: structured, time-stamped reports or drawings; clear denominator; explicit coding of null, ambiguous and positive reports.

### H2 — Cross-observer correspondence
Independent reports contain specific spatial/glyph-level correspondence beyond what generic visual motifs and reporting conventions would produce.

Appropriate evidence: frozen feature codebook, blinded scoring, inter-rater reliability, subject/session-level independence, comparison with matched control reports and a predeclared permutation baseline.

### H3 — Stimulus-locked structure
Features reliably vary with measured stimulus/optical properties rather than appearing equally in comparable control images or under alternative presentation conditions.

Appropriate evidence: characterized stimuli/images, safe archived or non-human optical data, predeclared image/feature statistics, and held-out stimuli.

### H4 — External code / extra information
The reports correspond to an independently defined external structure or convey information not available through the stimulus or ordinary channels.

This is a stronger claim. It requires a specific prospective prediction, an independently established ground truth, an information-flow analysis, a frozen score, and a confirmatory design. If the proposal gives no criterion by which a symbol sequence could be wrong, it is not yet a falsifiable empirical hypothesis.

**H1 does not imply H2; H2 does not imply H3; H1–H3 do not by themselves imply H4.** The simulation-hypothesis interpretation is separate again.

## Plausible mechanism families to compare

Do not pick a single explanation before the data distinguish them.

1. **Optical structure:** diffracted light and scattering can produce spatially structured, grainy/speckle patterns. The physical stimulus should be measured instead of assumed to be featureless.
2. **Visual-system dynamics:** altered gain/control and changes in visual processing can make edges, lattices, repetitive forms or geometric patterns more salient.
3. **Perceptual inference and expectation:** the brain interprets ambiguous signals; symbolic framing can influence descriptions and what features are selected for report.
4. **Cross-observer shared priors:** humans have similar visual systems and learned symbol categories, which can create convergent descriptions even when the external stimulus does not contain literal text.
5. **Unresolved alternatives:** a robust result not explained by these controls should be reported as unresolved, not automatically labelled as evidence for simulation, entities, or an exotic information source.

The aim is not to assert that one of these already fully explains the claim. It is to make them compete on evidence.

## Safe staged programme

### Stage A — Provenance/data-access audit

Before running image classifiers or matching reports, record:
- exact source versions and access dates;
- whether raw images/drawings, session-level records and stimulus data actually exist;
- study/experiment IDs, recruitment channel and number approached/enrolled/analysed;
- positive, null, ambiguous, missing and excluded counts;
- blinding and instructions shown to observers;
- source, consent and redistribution permissions;
- whether trials are independent or share participants, stimuli or sessions;
- any preregistration and deviations from it.

If participant-level materials cannot be lawfully accessed, the result is a documented blocker. Do not manufacture a dataset from public testimonials and call it replication.

### Stage B — Blind retrospective coding

Where rights and source materials permit:
1. redact condition/author/context metadata;
2. freeze a codebook for geometric structure, glyph segmentation, spatial relationships and confidence;
3. have independent raters annotate without knowing the source condition or the proposed “code” interpretation;
4. report agreement and disagreements, not only consensus items;
5. compare reports with shuffled-subject/session pairings, generic glyph corpora and matched synthetic controls;
6. predeclare allowed rotations/translations, alignment procedures, language matching and multiple-comparison handling.

A flexible matcher must not search thousands of alignments and then report only its best match. Any such search must be included in the null distribution.

### Stage C — Safe visual-control analysis

Use archived images or computational/simulated optical patterns to test whether a proposed feature metric is sensitive to:
- spatial frequency, contrast and edge density;
- diffractive or speckle-like texture;
- image blur, motion, and crop;
- generic glyph-like and random symbol arrays;
- label prompts and rater expectation.

Synthetic optical output is a pipeline/control test, not direct evidence about what any human experiences. Do not create instructions for DMT administration or for directing laser beams into eyes. No self-experiment or unsupervised recreation is part of this programme.

### Stage D — Prospective independent test gate

A future human study, especially one involving a controlled substance, is blocked until competent institutional/ethics review, authorization, privacy protections and optical safety approval. This repository task does not prescribe drug use, dose, sourcing, or an ocular-exposure method.

The protocol must specify:
- a single primary hypothesis and endpoint;
- a justified sample-size/power simulation;
- neutral versus suggestive instruction conditions where ethically and scientifically appropriate;
- a control group and a blinded analysis path;
- handling of negative/ambiguous/failed sessions;
- independently generated ground truth for any claimed extra-information endpoint;
- preregistration, data protection, all-trial accounting and independent replication.

## Metrics

Keep separate:
- report-data completeness and denominator accountability;
- inter-rater reliability;
- cross-observer similarity vs shuffled and matched controls;
- false-match rate on control images;
- robustness to feature definitions, alignment and language labels;
- sensitivity to instruction/expectation conditions;
- stimulus-feature correspondence;
- independent replication effect and uncertainty;
- for H4 only, performance against a predeclared external target/chance baseline and a conventional-information leakage audit.

There is no valid single “reality-code score” at this stage.

## Symthaea/Symtropy roles

- **Symthaea:** keep observation, interpretation and causal hypothesis separate; preserve alternatives and unknowns; create a versioned evidence bundle with exact source and annotation ancestry.
- **Symtropy:** generate safe synthetic visual-control families and test whether a frozen matching pipeline distinguishes them as expected.
- **Shared verification:** exact source/code/profile hashes, blind evaluation, complete failure/null accounting, no numeric fallback after errors.

A high similarity score or a model's fluent interpretation is not evidence that a symbol exists outside the observer or conveys external information.

## Claim ceiling

This programme can establish whether reported phenomenology is reproducible, whether reports exhibit blinded correspondence, and whether ordinary optical/perceptual models account for measured features. It cannot infer an external code or simulation solely from subjective reports. No new experiment or participant-data analysis has been run for this document.
