# DMC Individual-Effect Pilot: Reproduction Boundary and Symthaea Extension v0.1

**Status:** protocol recovery required. This document does not claim a reproduction or an executed model experiment.

## Why this pilot needs two tracks

Okita (2026-11-04) gives us a valuable diagnostic ladder for whether a task supports individual counterfactual effects and whether a world model preserves those effects from its latent state through imagined rollout. Its DMC `cartpole.swingup` formulation is a strong low-cost **environment-eligibility** candidate: 150 individuals, six action sequences, fifteen unordered pairs, twelve history blocks, and a paper-reported abducible share A=0.396 with estimation floor phi=0.093. The paper reports that formulation passing C1-C5 under its stated estimator and the source-specific A >= 3 phi convention.

There is a crucial boundary: the paper's Table 2 records **no trained world-model checkpoint** for the DMC formulation. DMC is used for the environment/observability diagnostic (including the Table 7 masking sweep), not for the paper's latent-versus-rollout model comparison. The model-side ladder is reported on other formulations. Therefore:

1. **Track A — reproduce the DMC environment-eligibility diagnostic.** This is the paper-replication target.
2. **Track B — train Symthaea models on the recovered DMC formulation.** This is a new research extension. It must not be described as reproducing a paper DMC world-model result.

This separation prevents us from comparing a new Symthaea model to a nonexistent paper DMC checkpoint.

## What the paper actually specifies

The DMC family contains `cartpole.swingup`, `cartpole.two_poles`, `cartpole.three_poles`, and `walker.walk`. Individuals are generated by perturbing physical-body parameters, including pole length. Six action sequences are applied from the same branch point, yielding fifteen pairs per individual. The history contains twelve blocks; the table gives b=12 and H=12. The swing-up diagnostic uses 150 individuals per task and five seeds.

The paper's Table 7 reports:

| Observation condition | Branch-point share | A | Seed SD | phi |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| None | 0.511 | 0.396 | 0.035 | 0.093 |
| Last-value probes only | 0.512 | 0.395 | 0.034 | 0.093 |
| Including pole angular velocity | 0.147 | 0.759 | 0.061 | 0.093 |
| Plus first-value probes | 0.108 | 0.800 | 0.080 | 0.093 |

The paper describes five mask rates and four orderings, for seventeen groups and five seeds each. The complete seventeen-group map and exact mask-rate/column schedule must be recovered from the supplementary materials or authors before reproduction. The four table rows above must not be silently treated as the entire 17-group design.

The estimator centres each individual contrast within its intervention pair. This removes the pair's population-average treatment effect and leaves the component varying between individuals. Without this centring, a predictor can score well by getting only the average ordering of interventions right. The paper reports a pooled-vs-centred correlation of 0.622 versus 0.029 in one example, illustrating how severe this estimand error can be.

C1-C5 are gates on the exact task/intervention/measurement formulation:
- **C1 action efficacy:** interventions must actually change the state/outcome.
- **C2 branchability:** the same individual and exogenous state can be restored and executed under multiple interventions.
- **C3 individual dependence:** the treatment effect must vary between individuals.
- **C4 measurability:** the environment's abducible share must be distinguishable from the estimator's finite-sample floor. The paper uses A >= 3 phi as its certification convention.
- **C5 convergence:** the estimate must stabilize as the number of individuals increases.

C1-C3 establish whether the individual-counterfactual question is posed. C4-C5 determine whether the measured share is readable. The three-times-floor threshold is a paper convention, not a universal statistical theorem; our preregistration must show at least 2x/3x/4x sensitivity and report the raw A and phi values.

The paper's data statement says its non-public-dataset data were generated by simulation, and that its generation scripts, generated datasets, and reproduction code would be deposited in a public repository upon publication. This audit has not located a linked repository for the DMC configuration. The exact physical-parameter distributions, action vectors, observation masks, seeds, reset driver, estimator settings and convergence rule are therefore still unresolved. We must obtain them before calling Track A a reproduction.

## Current upstream source pin is not paper parity

For an independently reproducible starting point, this pilot pins the current public `google-deepmind/dm_control` source at commit `d7e52e3602a555df6cc20cc928ea5b41e9920158` (package version 1.0.52; setup requires MuJoCo >=4.0.1). The exact `dm_control/suite/cartpole.py` blob is recorded in the JSON fixture.

At that pin, upstream `cartpole.swingup` uses the standard one-pole task, smooth reward and a 10-second default time limit. It randomizes initial position and small initial velocities. That is **not** equivalent to the paper's additional per-individual physical-body-parameter perturbations and six branch-point action sequences. The pin is a source baseline for a future adapter, not a replacement for missing paper settings.

## Track A — paper-faithful DMC eligibility replication

Do not train a Symthaea model until this audit is complete.

1. Recover the supplementary simulator config and, ideally, the authors' promised generator / dataset / figure-reproduction source. Compare the recovered version to the pinned current DMC implementation and explicitly record any changes.
2. Freeze and hash the full task configuration: XML/model constants, individual physical-parameter distributions and correlations, branch-state serializer, six action sequences, control scaling, block timing, reward/outcome aggregation, exogenous schedule, observation ordering/masks, all seeds and estimator parameters.
3. Generate each individual's common factual history once. At the branch point, restore the exact same individual state for all six interventions. Verify byte- or state-level factual-prefix equality. Share exogenous disturbances by stable event/tick identity; never force post-action endogenous state trajectories to remain equal.
4. Run C1, C2 and C3 as separate tests before attempting A. For C3, test the variance of within-pair-centred individual effects; do not infer heterogeneity from the population mean.
5. Estimate A and phi using the recovered paper estimator and split procedure. Resample individuals, not the fifteen pair rows, because all pairs from one individual share its history and hidden parameters.
6. Sweep nested individual cohort sizes and report the complete C5 convergence curve. Do not use a single 150-individual estimate as evidence of convergence.
7. Reproduce the Table 7 mask/order design and five seeds. Preserve the complete 17-group results even if the main paper table displays only selected condition rows.
8. Compare each result against paper values only after confirming exact configuration and estimator parity. Publish per-seed values, confidence intervals, floor, source digests, prefix-match receipts and failed runs.

The output of Track A is an environment-eligibility certificate and a reproducibility report—not a model capability or task-success result.

## Track B — Symthaea model-side extension

Once Track A passes, train and evaluate Symthaea on the same recovered physical individual and intervention definitions. This is where Symthaea can test a genuinely new model contribution: whether a typed HDC instance representation and action-conditioned CfC/LTC dynamics can preserve individual-specific treatment effects through imagination.

### Training hypothesis: make the contrast first-class

The paper already establishes prior art for a contrast-aware loss. Its intervention-trajectory loss adds a pairwise term:

`L = L_trajectory + lambda * L_individual-contrast`

where the second term penalizes the difference between predicted and realized outcomes for each intervention pair. The shared component cancels in a contrast, providing a direct gradient for the small intervention-dependent signal that a reconstruction loss can neglect. The paper also finds that checkpoint selection must attend to contrast validation; selecting only by reconstruction can choose a checkpoint whose rollout has lost the individual contrast.

Symthaea must not claim the contrast loss itself as novel. The proposed extension is to test its interaction with HDC/CfC/LTC and the full chain from instance-conditioned latent → branch-specific rollout → actual downstream decision, including a strict held-out individual and intervention-pair design.

Ablations:
1. Reconstruction-only.
2. Reconstruction plus action-conditioned transition prediction.
3. Reconstruction plus individual-contrast loss.
4. HDC-only with the same contrast objective.
5. CfC/LTC-only with the same contrast objective.
6. HDC+CfC/LTC with the same contrast objective.
7. A simple GRU/RSSM baseline, only where an implementation can be matched on data, parameter/compute budget and rollout interface.
8. A branch-point-only estimator and a history-only random-forest probe, plus a zero-effect predictor as diagnostic reference.
9. Checkpoint selected by reconstruction validation versus individual-contrast validation.
10. Frozen-latent readout versus the model's own action-conditioned imagined rollout.

The contrast objective must use **training branches only**. Validation individuals are used only for selection. Test individuals and the confirmatory intervention pairs stay sealed until the model and analysis are frozen. Keep all fifteen pairs from one individual inside one cohort split. Add a second generalization slice that holds out intervention pairs, and a third that holds out both individuals and intervention pairs.

### Minimum reporting contract

Report separate axes; no weighted leaderboard score.

- Environment eligibility: C1-C5, A, phi, threshold sensitivity and C5 convergence.
- Latent/rollout retention: A_lat, A_pred, A-A_lat and A_lat-A_pred, with the same estimator/floor semantics where identifiable.
- Individual calibration: centred MAE/RMSE versus zero-effect baseline, signed bias, sign accuracy, within-pair-centred correlation, slope, intercept and maximum bias over predeclared prediction bins.
- Action use: per-individual top-1 choice, regret under the declared utility, common epsilon-good action set through #13239/#13152, and actual full-episode decision value.
- Reliability: held-out-individual and held-out-pair performance, uncertainty/coverage, failure/non-application denominator, reset residual and branch replay completeness.
- Cost: model bytes, persistent memory, actual compute, simulator calls, p50/p95/p99 inference and application latency, and measured energy when calibrated instrumentation exists.

Use #8578 for cluster-aware inference over individuals. Do not bootstrap 15 pairs as though they were independent observations. Report per-seed variation separately from individual-cluster intervals. Do not fit a calibration transform on the confirmatory test set.

### Prospective targets (not results)

- The latent readout and imagined rollout both outperform the zero-effect predictor on held-out individuals.
- HDC+CfC/LTC beats its strongest single-component and simple-model baselines on the held-out individual-effect/decision frontier, without relying on a population mean.
- Where A_lat is above the estimation floor, an aspirational target is A_pred/A_lat >= 0.90; register this threshold before examining the confirmatory data.
- No improvement is accepted unless full-episode task performance and hard-constraint behavior do not regress beyond preregistered tolerances.
- Resource improvements are measured on the actual candidate path; vector dimension, FLOP estimates alone, or digest size are not sufficient evidence of physical memory/energy savings.
- Report negative results and regimes where a simpler baseline, full-state representation or no imagination wins.

## Industry comparison without denominator errors

This DMC pilot is a mechanism-level diagnostic, not a replacement for standard world-model benchmarks.

- **Mechanism track:** Track A and Track B above. Compare methods on the exact same restored individuals, interventions, training labels and held-out splits.
- **Closed-loop control/robotics track:** use #9094/#13389 with the official π0.5, X-VLA and FastWAM baselines where assets and launchers can be recovered. Match tasks, checkpoint, observation budget, action candidates, simulator calls, wall-clock deadline and compute. Report success, progress, application-time regret, recovery, latency and resource vectors separately. Never pool standard LIBERO, LIBERO-Dynamic and RoboDyna scores.
- **Branching prior art:** Rollback already establishes experience backtracking, action-chunk alternatives and branch-outcome supervision. Compare against it as prior art, not as something Symthaea newly invents.
- **Physical track:** only advance to HIL after measured state restoration residuals, tolerances and independent reset receipts are available. Randomized matched physical units support subgroup/population effect estimates, not exact per-unit potential outcomes.

A result is stronger than SOTA only when a matched, independently reproducible comparison shows a Pareto improvement: better individual counterfactual fidelity and decision quality at equal or lower compute/latency/energy, with no hard-constraint regression. No cross-benchmark percentage or mismatched paper number can establish that claim.
