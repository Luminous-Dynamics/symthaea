# REL — Automorphism Census

**Status:** analytic companion to REL-001/002/003. This note does not change production cognition and does not promote representation symmetry to field-theoretic gauge symmetry.

## Purpose

REL separates three increasingly strong questions:

1. which transformations preserve binding algebra;
2. which also preserve the representation metric;
3. which additionally preserve a fixed sequence operator rather than merely transforming covariantly with it.

The point is to derive the admissible transformation family from Symthaea's actual representation structure instead of importing `O(d)` or `GL(d)` from a different architecture.

## BinaryHV

Treat `BinaryHV` as the vector space `F_2^n` with XOR binding.

### XOR algebra alone

Automorphisms of the additive group `(F_2^n, XOR)` are the invertible `F_2`-linear maps:

`G_bind = GL(n, 2)`.

This is intentionally broader than the cognitively stronger symmetry family because a generic invertible binary linear map need not preserve Hamming geometry.

### XOR plus Hamming geometry

A linear Hamming isometry must send every weight-one vector to another weight-one vector. Therefore it permutes the standard basis, so the intersection of XOR automorphisms and Hamming isometries is represented by permutation matrices:

`G_bind+metric ~= S_n`.

The REL binary shear negative control witnesses the strict separation: it is invertible and XOR-linear while changing Hamming distance.

## ContinuousHV

For the declared linear representation structure, model `ContinuousHV` as the real coordinate algebra `(R^n, +, Hadamard)` with cosine geometry.

Under the explicit restriction to **linear unital algebra automorphisms**, the primitive coordinate idempotents must be permuted. Thus the admitted algebra automorphisms are coordinate permutations:

`G_linear-algebra ~= S_n`.

Those permutations also preserve Euclidean inner products, norms, and cosine similarity, so they survive the metric intersection.

This statement does **not** classify arbitrary nonlinear maps and does not claim that every cosine isometry is a Hadamard-algebra automorphism. REL's orthogonal-mixing negative control explicitly demonstrates the latter failure.

## Sequence operator

Let `rho` be Symthaea's one-step cyclic coordinate shift. Once `rho` is treated as a fixed semantic operator, the relevant symmetry family is the centralizer of `rho` inside the coordinate-permutation group:

`G_fixed-sequence = { g in S_n : g rho = rho g }`.

For a single full `n`-cycle, that centralizer is the cyclic subgroup generated by the shift itself:

`G_fixed-sequence ~= <rho> ~= C_n`.

So adding a fixed sequence operator sharply reduces the symmetry admitted by the binding-plus-metric representation.

By contrast, a general coordinate-frame change remains covariant if the operator changes with the frame:

`rho' = g rho g^-1`.

Therefore:

`fixed-operator symmetry != representation covariance`.

## Implementation consequences

The next REL qualification layer should test all of the following separately:

- general deterministic coordinate permutations as binding-plus-metric symmetries;
- permutation identity, inverse, and composition laws as a group action;
- bundle covariance under common coordinate permutations;
- powers of the production cyclic shift as positive fixed-operator symmetries;
- a non-commuting coordinate permutation as a negative fixed-operator control;
- conjugated sequence operators as a positive covariance control.

## Claim boundary

This census establishes an analytic target under the stated algebraic assumptions. Executable Symthaea qualification must still verify that production implementations realize those identities. It does not establish improved cognition, a useful quotient representation, a gauge field, or consciousness.
