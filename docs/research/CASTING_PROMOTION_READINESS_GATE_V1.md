# CASTING-0037 — Evidence-class isolation and fail-closed promotion readiness v1

**Issue:** [CASTING-0037 / #8866](https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symthaea/issues/8866)  
**Implementation branch:** `research/casting-promotion-readiness-gate-2026-10-29`  
**Status:** research-stage admission gate, not deployment approval or a physical safety certificate.

## Why one more gate is needed

A paired comparison can emit point estimates and intervals, but an analyst still needs to decide whether the evidence is sufficient to advance a policy. This should be a versioned, executable contract—not an informal reading of a green report. The policy may only advance when the required evidence class, frozen methodology, comparison effects, critical-family regressions, budget accounting, and independent false-feasible validation all satisfy predeclared rules.

A 2026 review of computational modeling, AI and digital twins in casting highlights open problems in standardized benchmarking, quantified uncertainty, cross-alloy generalization, and production-stage validation: https://doi.org/10.1007/s43939-026-00685-5. The gate is designed to prevent a reproducible synthetic score from being interpreted as evidence of physical process qualification.

## 1. Evidence classes never pool silently

Every paired manifest declares one `evidence_class`:

- `synthetic`: analytically generated research fixtures;
- `public_analytic`: public analytical benchmark problems under an identified suite revision;
- `simulator_backed`: outcomes generated by a named, versioned engineering simulator;
- `physical`: independently collected physical measurements.

The class flows into the paired receipt. A promotion contract must require exactly the class it is intended to advance. A `synthetic` receipt cannot satisfy a `simulator_backed` or `physical` requirement. These classes may be reported side-by-side in a portfolio, but cannot be concatenated into one apparent sample size or one pooled score.

The class string is an operator assertion: validating a label does not authenticate the dataset behind it. Retain and verify the source artifacts and their digests independently.

## 2. Correctly count wins, ties and families

CASTING-0035 now reports both:
- `win_rate_all_units = wins / all independent paired units`, where ties are explicitly non-wins; and
- `win_rate_excluding_ties`, a secondary descriptive statistic whose denominator omits tied pairs.

The frozen "at least 70% of tasks won" target uses the **all-unit** rate. The paired bootstrap interval resamples whole independent `unit_id` pairs, preserving policy pairing and never treating candidate × shift rows as independent evidence.

For each task family, the paired receipt also reports relative AUC improvement and its bootstrap interval, excluding families/units with zero baseline AUC from the relative denominator and explicitly counting those exclusions. A critical family with insufficient pairs or zero-baseline exclusions is inconclusive for promotion—not silently dropped.

A percentile bootstrap is a descriptive empirical uncertainty procedure, not a universal guarantee. Tiny samples can produce degenerate intervals; independent-unit declarations and the sampling design must be justified before the test. The contract must preregister a minimum number of independent task units and a minimum per critical family.

## 3. Promotion contract

The CLI consumes three strict UTF-8 JSON files:

```sh
python3 tools/casting-benchmark/paired_benchmark_promotion_gate.py \
  paired-receipt.json promotion-contract.json independent-validation.json \
  --output promotion-readiness.json
```

The contract freezes:
- candidate and strongest reproduced baseline policy IDs;
- required evidence class and exact benchmark/validation protocol SHA-256 digests;
- minimum total independent task units, minimum per critical family, minimum independent validation units, and minimum paired target reaches;
- minimum median relative cost-normalized anytime AUC improvement;
- minimum all-unit win rate, counting ties as non-wins;
- maximum permitted relative AUC regression in every named critical family;
- minimum cost-to-target reduction on jointly reaching pairs;
- maximum false-feasible rate and one-sided confidence level;
- whether incomplete or over-budget runs disqualify promotion.

The contract is strict-schema. Unknown keys, malformed values, duplicated critical-family names and invalid digests fail rather than being ignored.

## 4. Independent validation input and risk bound

The independent validation manifest must name one unique `unit_id` per independent validation unit. Each record contains task family, shift ID, the policy's predicted-feasibility boolean, observed-feasibility boolean, a decision-receipt digest and an outcome-receipt digest. Duplicate unit IDs or decision/outcome digests fail closed.

The gate requires the validation protocol digest and candidate policy source/configuration digests to match the frozen contract and paired receipt. It recomputes the false-feasible count from records rather than trusting a caller-supplied `certified=true` value. The denominator is the number of independently held-out records where the candidate actually predicted feasible; false-feasible means predicted feasible but observed infeasible.

For `x` false-feasible outcomes among `n` predicted-feasible independent validation units, the gate recomputes the one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson upper confidence bound and compares it with the frozen risk target. Zero predictions of feasibility gives no risk evidence and yields an inconclusive result, not a zero-risk claim.

This calculation assumes the declared independent validation units represent an exchangeable population. The code cannot prove that assumption, verify physical instrumentation, or establish that the holdout was actually hidden. A manifest's `independent_held_out` label and freeze booleans are claims whose backing protocol and evidence must be audited.

## 5. Decision states

- `ready_for_next_stage`: every configured criterion passes; this permits only the next predeclared validation stage, not deployment or process certification.
- `not_promoted`: sufficient data was provided for at least some checks, but one or more thresholds fail.
- `inconclusive`: required independent samples, critical-family results, validation trials, or eligible target-reaching pairs are insufficient.
- `wrong_evidence_class`: the supplied paired results or validation are not from the evidence class required by the contract.

Invalid schemas, inconsistent policy identities, mismatched protocol digests, and policy source/config mismatches are hard validation errors. These are distinct from a scientifically inconclusive experiment.

The gate checks both the point estimate and the 95% percentile bootstrap interval against the configured effect threshold for the main relative AUC, all-unit win rate, critical-family regression, and jointly reached cost-to-target reduction. These are intentionally stringent promotion conditions. If the study is not powered to satisfy them, increase the preregistered number of independent tasks or call the result inconclusive; do not lower thresholds after inspecting results.

## 6. Required negative controls

The standard-library suite covers:
- enough evidence to pass, and each evidence-class mismatch;
- ties counted as non-wins and target gates not met;
- sample size too small, missing critical-family results, incomplete runs and over-budget runs;
- a risk sample that exceeds the false-feasible bound and a sample with no predicted-feasible cases;
- candidate source/config mismatch, validation protocol mismatch, duplicate validation units and receipt digests;
- unknown evidence class, duplicate critical families, invalid targets, duplicate JSON keys and attempted input overwrite.

Next hardening should independently verify the policy source/environment artifacts named by their digests; archive the frozen contract and run receipts before holdout unsealing; and obtain external proof-of-existence for the pre-outcome record. These are not implied by this evaluator.

## 7. Benchmark success targets are still targets, not results

The prior CASTING-0032 aims remain:
- at least 5% median relative cost-normalized feasible-quality AUC improvement over the strongest reproduced baseline;
- wins on at least 70% of all preregistered independent task units, with ties as non-wins;
- no more than 5% median relative regression on each critical task family;
- at least 20% lower cost to the same quality target on sufficiently numerous pairs where both methods reach that target.

The gate also requires an independent false-feasible upper bound below the predeclared limit. No optimizer is claimed to have passed these targets until the exact-head CI is green, sufficient matched-cost experiments are complete, every failure is published, and the appropriate evidence class has been independently checked.

Industrial platforms such as MAGMASOFT already offer autonomous casting optimization and robust process-window analysis. The candidate differentiator is not generic optimization; it is a reproducible end-to-end evidence contract that makes the cost, uncertainty, shift-specific failure, abstention, and promotion decision auditable.
