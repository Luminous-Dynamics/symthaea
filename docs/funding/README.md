## Verifiable, Interpretable, Low-Power Cognitive Architectures

Luminous Dynamics is an independent research lab developing **Symthaea**, an open-source Rust cognitive architecture. We are building the foundational software for a new generation of autonomous systems that are mathematically verifiable, energy-efficient, and aligned with human values.

### The Problem
Current AI systems (mostly Transformers) are opaque "black boxes," computationally expensive, and difficult to verify for safety-critical applications. They lack a grounded model of their own physical substrate and have no formal path to verifiable autonomy.

### The Solution: Symthaea
Symthaea departs from the transformer paradigm, using three converged technologies:
1.  **Hyperdimensional Computing (HDC):** Representing concepts as 16,384-bit vectors to enable robust, efficient, and interpretable symbolic logic.
2.  **Active Inference:** A mathematical framework (Free Energy Principle) that allows agents to minimize uncertainty and optimize behavior through self-organized perception and action.
3.  **Verifiable Logic:** Leveraging binary-field STARKs to generate cryptographic proofs of neural updates and concept-binding operations.

### Research Tracks
- **Track 1: Verifiable AI (ZK-HDC)**
   Cryptographic proofs for 16,384-bit concept binding. Optimized for binary-field arithmetic where XOR operations are "free."
- **Track 2: Efficient Autonomy (HAI)**
   Hyperdimensional Active Inference for low-power edge robotics. Outperforms standard probabilistic models in efficiency and scaling.
- **Track 3: Consciousness Evaluation**
   A testbed for evaluating global workspace broadcast, integrated information peaks, and epistemic stability in non-transformer architectures.
