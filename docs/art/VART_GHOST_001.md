# VART-GHOST-001 — Live Four-Ghost Artistic Loop

Status: preregistered before the first live confirmatory four-render run.

## Question

Can one persistent artistic system inspect one committed Bevy scene, render a do-nothing baseline plus exactly three revision-bound proposal ghosts, measure their visual consequences on one synchronized observation plane, make a non-scalar artistic decision, and close the selected/non-selected causal loop without contaminating the committed scene?

This study tests studio mechanics and causal evidence. It does **not** test subjective enjoyment or universal artistic quality.

## Frozen unit

One confirmatory unit contains exactly:

- one committed base revision and semantic scene hash;
- one studio frame;
- one stable camera identity;
- one render-fidelity profile;
- one artistic intention/question reference;
- one committed baseline / abstention candidate;
- exactly three unique proposal IDs and three unique preview branch IDs;
- exactly four completed render observations;
- one multidimensional visual-consequence record per proposal;
- one decision: SelectProposal, Abstain, Revise, or Inconclusive;
- one causal closure receipt.

No candidate may be silently removed after observation begins.

## Observation-plane rule

All four candidates share the same:

- base revision;
- base committed scene hash;
- studio frame;
- camera identity;
- render fidelity.

The three proposal ghosts are expected to have candidate-specific rendered semantic scene hashes when the proposed intervention changes the scene. Requiring proposal pixels to claim the committed base hash would erase the very counterfactual distinction being measured.

## Visual evidence

The first qualification uses inexpensive deterministic whole-frame dimensions:

- mean luminance;
- RMS contrast;
- edge energy;
- mean saturation;
- horizontal luminance balance;
- vertical luminance balance;
- center emphasis.

These are descriptive perception channels, not an aesthetic score. Candidate-minus-baseline deltas remain separate.

Later Vision-Manifold evidence may add silhouette, depth, focal hierarchy, semantic relationships, motion, and temporal structure, but cannot replace the frozen RT milestone receipt.

## Fail-closed capture rule

A dropped, evicted, duplicated, cross-frame, cross-revision, cross-camera, mixed-fidelity, or otherwise invalid capture invalidates/incompletes the four-ghost unit. It must not become a three-candidate study.

## Decision rule

A decision may select one proposal, abstain, request revision of the alternatives, or remain inconclusive.

Selection is **not commit authority**. The normal `symthaea-art-world` authority boundary still governs any real mutation.

No universal beauty, utility, acceptance, reward, or weighted-sum score is introduced by VART-GHOST-001.

## Causal closure gates

For a selected proposal:

1. the committed proposal ID must equal the selected proposal ID;
2. the separately authorized deterministic commit must reproduce the selected preview's semantic scene hash;
3. all three preview branches must be disposed;
4. the decision, visual evidence, and closure must bind to the same base revision/frame.

For Abstain, Revise, or Inconclusive:

1. no proposal may be committed;
2. the post-cycle committed semantic scene hash must equal the original base hash;
3. all three preview branches must be disposed.

## First live qualification

The first live run should use a deliberately simple known 3D scene and three visibly distinct but bounded proposals, such as:

- camera displacement;
- light displacement/intensity change;
- form transform or material change.

The purpose is to prove the complete causal loop before testing artistic sophistication.

## Pass criteria

A confirmatory unit passes the studio-mechanics gate only if:

- exactly four non-empty GPU observations complete;
- all observation-plane bindings validate;
- all four visual observations are analyzed;
- the three proposal consequence vectors are produced without scalarization;
- the decision receipt validates;
- preview creation/rendering leaves the committed scene unchanged before authorization;
- the causal closure rule above passes;
- no capture/readback/backpressure failure is hidden.

A passed mechanics gate does not mean the artistic decision was aesthetically good. It means Symthaea can perform a causally traceable observe-imagine-see-decide-act/abstain loop in a living 3D studio.
