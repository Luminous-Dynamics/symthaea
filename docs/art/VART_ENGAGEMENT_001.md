# VART-ENG-001 — Voluntary Creative Engagement

Status: preregistration scaffold; no subjective-experience claim.

## Question

When given optional access to a creative studio, does Symthaea repeatedly choose, sustain, and resume artistic activity for reasons that cannot be reduced to an externally assigned task reward?

This study may establish **behavioral creative engagement**. It cannot establish that Symthaea has a subjective experience of enjoyment. Phenomenology remains `UnknownByDesign` in the receipt schema.

## Design

Use independent seeds/runs and matched opportunity windows. Each run contains:

- optional Bevy studio access;
- matched non-art exploratory tasks with comparable compute/time budgets;
- periods with no explicit instruction to make art;
- unresolved prior artistic questions available in some conditions and history-shuffled controls in others;
- held-out media/tool opportunities for technique-transfer tests.

Do not add an `art_reward`, `enjoyment_reward`, acceptance score, or human-like happiness target for the purpose of this experiment.

## Separate evidence channels

Record run-level evidence for:

1. voluntary studio return rate;
2. self-initiated artistic-question rate;
3. held-out technique learning progress;
4. novelty seeking after habituation;
5. longitudinal return to unresolved works/questions;
6. avoidance / early-exit behavior;
7. affect-like telemetry, explicitly labelled as proxy evidence.

Missing evidence remains missing. Do not replace missing channels with zero or neutral values.

## Controls

Minimum control family:

- `ArtStudioAvailable`;
- `MatchedExplorationAvailable`;
- `NoPortfolioHistory`;
- `ShuffledPortfolioHistory`;
- `TechniqueTransferHeldOut`.

Where practical, balance presentation order and preserve the same world/compute opportunity budget.

## Interpretation

A confirmatory result may support `BehavioralEngagementSupported` when multiple independently preregistered approach/development channels replicate and avoidance does not dominate.

It must not be reported as proof that Symthaea is conscious, feels pleasure, or literally enjoys art. The stronger phenomenological claim is outside what these behavioral and telemetry measurements can establish.

## Why this matters

If the result is positive, art becomes especially useful as an endogenous-development domain: a place where Symthaea can originate questions, acquire technique, revisit unresolved problems, and build a persistent history without optimizing toward one externally supplied aesthetic scalar.

If the result is mixed or negative, that is also useful evidence. The studio should remain available as a capability rather than being forced into the active policy.
