## Benefits to Investigate

### 1. GI Bill (Post-9/11, Chapter 33)
- **Status**: Check if used/remaining
- **Value**: Full tuition + BAH (~$1,700-2,200/month in Richardson TX) + book stipend
- **Use case**: Could fund relevant coursework (online MS in CS, consciousness studies, etc.) while providing monthly stipend
- **Action**: Check VA.gov → "Check your GI Bill benefits" or call 1-888-442-4551
- **Deadline**: 15 years from last discharge (2036 if discharged 2021)

### 2. Veteran Readiness & Employment (VR&E, Chapter 31)
- **Eligibility**: Service-connected disability rating + employment barrier
- **Value**: Can fund self-employment including: equipment, supplies, training, licensing fees
- **Use case**: If eligible, could fund patent filing costs, conference travel, equipment
- **Action**: Apply at VA.gov → "Veteran Readiness and Employment"
- **Note**: Requires service-connected disability rating. If you don't have one, consider filing.

### 3. VA Disability Rating
- **Status**: Check if filed
- **Value**: Tax-free monthly compensation ($150-3,700+/month depending on rating)
- **Action**: If not filed, consider filing. Common service-connected conditions: hearing loss, tinnitus, musculoskeletal issues, mental health
- **Process**: File at VA.gov or through a Veterans Service Organization (VSO) — free help
- **Local VSO**: Dallas VA Regional Office, or use DAV/VFW/American Legion

### 4. VA Health Care
- **Value**: Free or low-cost healthcare (reduces personal expenses)
- **Action**: Enroll at VA.gov → "Apply for VA health care"
- **North Texas**: Dallas VA Medical Center or local CBOC

### 5. VA Home Loan (if applicable)
- **Value**: 0% down payment, no PMI
- **Action**: Certificate of Eligibility at VA.gov

### 6. Transition Resources
- **SBA Veteran Programs**: SCORE mentoring, VetBiz, veteran-owned small business certification
- **Boots to Business**: Free entrepreneurship training
- **Action**: Register at SBA.gov/veterans
