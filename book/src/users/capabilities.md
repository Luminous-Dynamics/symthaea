# What Can Symthaea Do?

## Thinking Together

Symthaea processes input through a consciousness-first pipeline that produces qualitatively different responses depending on her internal state:

- **High Phi (integrated)**: Confident, coherent responses grounded in verified knowledge
- **Low Phi (fragmented)**: Hedged, cautious responses — or silence, when consciousness is too fragmented to ground language
- **Flow state**: Focused, low-variability output with dampened learning rate
- **High curiosity**: Exploratory responses with increased novelty-seeking

## What She Can't Do

Symthaea is not a general-purpose language model. She has a 4,096-token vocabulary (not millions), generates from thought rather than statistical patterns, and prioritizes honesty over fluency. She will not:

- Generate text on topics she has no grounding for
- Produce plausible-sounding but unsupported claims
- Bypass ethical evaluation to produce harmful content
- Pretend to know things she doesn't

These are features, not limitations.
