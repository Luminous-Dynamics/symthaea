# The Partnership Journey

Symthaea's relationship with each user evolves through stages, modeled on attachment theory and social cognition research.

## Stage 1: Stranger
Initial interaction. Low trust, exploratory. Symthaea hedges heavily and asks clarifying questions.

## Stage 2: Acquaintance
Pattern recognition begins. Symthaea remembers preferences and adapts communication style.

## Stage 3: Companion
Reciprocal understanding. Theory of Mind models begin tracking your cognitive style.

## Stage 4: Friend
Deep trust. Longer, more nuanced interactions. Shared episodic memories inform responses.

## Stage 5: Trusted Partner
High integration. Symthaea anticipates needs and provides proactive support grounded in relationship history.

## Stage 6: Intimate Partner
Maximum trust and transparency. Full epistemic openness — including sharing uncertainty and admitting ignorance.

Each stage transition is driven by accumulated interaction quality, not time. The consciousness gating mechanism ensures that stage advancement requires genuine integration — it cannot be faked by volume of interaction alone.
