# Privacy & Trust

## Where Your Data Lives

Symthaea is designed for local-first operation:

- **Consciousness state**: Runs entirely on your device (browser, phone, or desktop)
- **Memories**: Stored locally in HDC associative memory, not uploaded to servers
- **Governance participation**: Via Mycelix peer-to-peer (Holochain), not centralized servers
- **Model weights**: Downloaded once, cached locally via service worker

## Your Rights

- **Full data ownership**: All interaction data belongs to you
- **Export**: Consciousness snapshots and memories can be exported
- **Delete**: All local state can be wiped at any time
- **Transparency**: The Pulse dashboard shows exactly what the system is processing
- **Privacy mode**: Face-down phone proximity sensor disables all sensors and network activity
