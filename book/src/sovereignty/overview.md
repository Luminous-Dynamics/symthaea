Twenty-one essays arguing that the alignment problem is not a future risk of AGI but a 400-year-old design flaw in human coordination technology — and that consciousness coupling is the minimum viable mechanism for fixing it.

## The Argument in One Paragraph

The VOC charter of 1602 created a coordination architecture — perpetual existence, limited liability, transferable shares, delegated power — that severs the feedback loop between governance and consequence. DAOs replicate this architecture on a blockchain. The alternative is consciousness coupling: governance power proportional to a participant's measurable consciousness of the governed domain, implemented through a four-dimensional credential (identity, reputation, community, engagement), five progressive tiers, and fractal scale-invariant architecture. The system is defended against Sybil attacks, plutocratic capture, and algorithmic tyranny. It operates with epistemic humility (0.10 confidence in its own substrate's consciousness), precautionary inclusion, and radical transparency.
