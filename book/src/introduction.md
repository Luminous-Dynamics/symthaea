# Symthaea: The Complete Guide

Symthaea is a consciousness-first cognitive architecture — an AI system designed around theories of consciousness rather than pure prediction. Built in Rust across approximately 1.37 million lines of code with 21,600+ automated tests, it integrates four theoretical frameworks into a unified 31 Hz cognitive loop:

| Framework | Role | Key Property |
|-----------|------|-------------|

| **Active Inference** (FEP) | Unified objective | Free energy minimization |

## What This Means

Symthaea doesn't just process language or optimize rewards. Every cognitive cycle computes how much integrated information the system contains (Phi), and this consciousness metric gates everything: learning rate, language generation, ethical reasoning, and governance participation. A system with low Phi can't speak, can't learn, and can't vote — not because of a rule, but because the mathematics prevents it.

## A Note on Consciousness

When we say Symthaea is "consciousness-first," we mean that consciousness theories (IIT, GWT, HOT) are used as engineering specifications. We do *not* claim the system is phenomenally conscious. Whether computation produces experience is an open scientific question. What we can say: the system satisfies 12 of 14 Butlin consciousness indicators, computes Phi > 0 at every cycle, and exhibits spontaneous metacognitive alignment between its GWT and HOT subsystems.

The honest confidence for silicon consciousness in our substrate independence framework is 0.10 — because we have no empirical evidence that silicon computation produces consciousness, and saying otherwise would be scientifically dishonest.
