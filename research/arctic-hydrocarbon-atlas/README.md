# Arctic Hydrocarbon Evidence Atlas v0

**Status: S1 cited transcription only. Source bytes have not been downloaded or hashed by this workstream. This is not a resource/reserve re-assessment, drilling plan or development recommendation.**

The 2008 USGS Circum-Arctic Resource Appraisal is a useful published historical baseline: it assessed undiscovered conventional resources across 33 geological provinces north of the Arctic Circle. Its aggregate mean estimates are not proved or economic reserves, not current production, and not amounts located at the geographic North Pole.

- [USGS Fact Sheet 2008-3049](https://pubs.usgs.gov/fs/2008/3049/)
- [USGS Publications Warehouse record](https://www.usgs.gov/publications/circum-arctic-resource-appraisal-estimates-undiscovered-oil-and-gas-north-arctic)
- [UNCLOS, Articles 76–77: continental shelf and coastal-state rights](https://www.un.org/depts/los/convention_agreements/texts/unclos/part6.htm)
- [U.S.–Canada Beaufort Sea boundary task force statement (2024)](https://2021-2025.state.gov/joint-statement-on-creation-of-joint-task-force-to-negotiate-beaufort-sea-boundary/)
- [Arctic Council: Arctic oil-spill response challenges](https://arctic-council.org/news/data-improve-oil-spill-response-in-arctic/)

## Principles

1. Keep undiscovered resources, discoveries, technically recoverable quantities, proved reserves, production, economics and authorizations separate.
2. Keep geography and jurisdiction explicit; never map a circum-Arctic aggregate to the geographic North Pole.
3. Preserve the 2008 vintage, units, statistic and methodology; don't imply it's a current reserve estimate.
4. Analyze development alternatives as scenarios, with energy security, lifecycle emissions, methane, infrastructure, spill readiness, ecological effects, Indigenous/local rights and alternatives visible separately.
5. Hard legal, safety and rights constraints cannot be traded away by a single economic score.
6. Promote from S1 only after source-file ingestion and hashes, independent reproduction and the relevant domain review.
