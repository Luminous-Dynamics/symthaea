# S3 permutation-action exact benchmark v0.1

This companion fixture expands the exact subgroup/stabilizer benchmark beyond planar point groups.

## Action

The ambient group is (S_3), acting on (mathbb{R}^3) by coordinate permutations of the seed point ((11,23,47)). The seed has distinct coordinates, so its stabilizer in (S_3) is trivial. For every subgroup (H leq S_3), the finite orbit (H \cdot x) therefore has exact stabilizer (H).

The complete subgroup lattice contains six distinct subgroups:
- trivial (C_1);
- three distinct order-two subgroups generated by the three transpositions;
- one cyclic order-three subgroup (C_3);
- the full (S_3).

All six orbit sets are frozen in `fixtures/s3_permutation_v0.1.json`. Group elements, all 36 products and inverse witnesses are declared explicitly and recomputed by the Rust oracle.

## Exact Rust oracle

The standalone crate `permutation-oracle`:
- checks the complete permutation group, identity, inverses, closure and associativity;
- exhaustively enumerates all 64 element subsets and identifies the six subgroups;
- verifies that all six frozen point orbits have exactly the declared stabilizers;
- audits proposed subgroups and returns exact moved-orbit-point counterexamples;
- rejects forged receipts on independent replay.

The adversarial proposal fixture distinguishes:
- valid group but false object symmetry: proposing full (S_3) for the (C_3) orbit yields three false promotions;
- valid but incomplete subgroup: proposing (C_2) for the full (S_3) orbit misses four symmetries;
- non-subgroup proposal: identity plus a single 3-cycle is rejected for missing inverse/closure.

## Claim boundary

This is an exact finite permutation-action fixture. It does not establish discovery performance, unknown continuous group recovery, statistical sample complexity, physical symmetry, dynamics, or SOTA superiority. It is an additional benchmark family, not a performance result.
