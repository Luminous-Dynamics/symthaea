# Patch 0018: feat add maintainer rotation and stewardship handoff

**Series:** 29

## Objective

Reduce bus factor without confusing software stewardship with evidence authority.

## Intended changes

- Document repository access, release procedures, verifier fixtures, signing responsibilities for software releases, private disclosure access, and archive custody.
- Require handoff rehearsal and revocation of obsolete repository credentials.
- Keep catalog publication authorities outside ordinary maintainer privileges.

## Required evidence

- A new maintainer can reproduce a maintenance release from the runbooks.
- Obsolete software-release credentials are revoked.
- Maintainer access cannot authorize evidence publication or reverse retirement.

## Non-claims

- Does not guarantee organizational continuity.
- Does not expose private evidence keys.
