# Post-Release Stewardship and Regression-Prevention Contract

A Series 29 release line may claim maintenance readiness only when:

1. Support and compatibility promises are explicit and machine readable.
2. Regression intake and triage preserve reproducible evidence.
3. Every confirmed defect produces a frozen fixture.
4. Corrective patches remain minimal and evidence linked.
5. Backports are tested independently on each supported branch.
6. Security reports follow a bounded coordinated-disclosure workflow.
7. Dependency, clean-room reproduction, independent-verifier, soak, and fault lanes run on the declared cadence.
8. Deprecations preserve historical verification and explicit migration windows.
9. Every maintenance release carries a reproducible evidence bundle.
10. Emergency software releases cannot skip hard semantic, privacy, or transaction gates.
11. Maintainer handoff remains separate from catalog publication authority.
12. Seeded maintenance rehearsals prove the process can operate end to end.

This contract does not promise indefinite support, zero vulnerabilities, or perfect future operations.
