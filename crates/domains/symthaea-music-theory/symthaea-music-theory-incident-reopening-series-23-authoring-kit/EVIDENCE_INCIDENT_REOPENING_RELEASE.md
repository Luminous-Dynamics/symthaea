# Incident Reopening, Recurrence, and Segment-Freeze Release Contract

A Series 23 release may claim governed incident reopening only when:

1. Later evidence is accepted first as a bounded challenge, not as an authoritative fact.
2. Referenced evidence is verified through its native contracts under verifier-owned trigger policy.
3. Technical trigger satisfaction is reported separately from the governance decision to reopen.
4. The reopening plan binds the exact incident, closure, active segment, catalog head, challenge ledger, adverse-evidence report, policy epochs, and freeze point.
5. Active recovery authorities and recovered witnesses authenticate the reopening plan under expected policy.
6. Reopening, segment freeze, quarantine changes, and receipts commit atomically.
7. Publication mutation paths recheck segment state and reject all writes after committed freeze.
8. Prior closure, resumption, and publication history remain immutable and visible.
9. Recurrence links relate incidents without merging their identities or asserting blame.
10. Portable review packages distinguish submission, corroboration, authorization, and committed freeze.

This contract does not claim every allegation is true, all hidden branches are detectable, reopening proves wrongdoing, or recurrence proves common human responsibility.
