# Terminal Retirement and Archive-Only Release Contract

A Series 25 release may claim terminal retirement only when:

1. A verifier-owned trust-exhaustion policy and exact technical trigger report are available.
2. A canonical retirement plan binds the complete current incident, cycle, segment, authority, witness, quarantine, delegation, allowance, and preservation state.
3. The required governance roles authenticate that exact plan under expected active policies.
4. Retirement reauthenticates all mutable state and commits atomically.
5. Every catalog-lineage mutation path rejects the retired identity after commit.
6. Publisher delegations and allowances are terminally revoked.
7. Authority and witness rotation become historical-verification-only.
8. Read-only verification, preservation, and disclosure remain available in archive-only mode.
9. A terminal checkpoint binds the final catalog and complete lifecycle history.
10. Any successor uses a new identity and makes continuity claims explicitly rather than inheriting authority.
11. Public retirement packages are privacy bounded and independently reproducible.
12. Archive custody remains separate from publication authority.

This contract does not claim physical key destruction, permanent storage, universal canonicality, legal fault, or successor trustworthiness.
