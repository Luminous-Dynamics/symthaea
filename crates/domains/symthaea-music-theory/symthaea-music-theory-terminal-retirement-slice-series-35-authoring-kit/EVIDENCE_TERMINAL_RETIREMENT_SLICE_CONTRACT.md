1. The Series 34 active two-cycle lineage passes all native audits.
2. A verifier-owned trust-exhaustion policy produces an exact technical report.
3. A canonical retirement plan inventories every active mutation capability and archive obligation.
4. The configured governance roles authenticate that exact plan under expected policies.
5. All mutable state is reauthenticated at commit time.
6. Retirement, revocations, terminal policy events, archive mode, checkpoint, and receipt commit atomically.
7. Every authoritative mutation path rejects the retired lineage after commit.
8. Delegations and allowances are terminally revoked.
9. Authority and witness policies become historical-verification-only.
10. Read-only verification, preservation, disclosure, and custody remain available.
11. Any successor uses a new identity and receives no implicit authority continuity.
12. Public retirement packages are privacy bounded and reproducible.
13. Native and independent vectors agree.
14. Race, crash, rollback, archive, privacy, observer, and successor corpora pass.

This contract does not prove physical key destruction, permanent storage, universal canonicality, legal fault, or successor trustworthiness.
