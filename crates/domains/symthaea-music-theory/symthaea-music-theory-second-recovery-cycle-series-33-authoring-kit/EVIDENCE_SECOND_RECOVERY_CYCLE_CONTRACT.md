# Second Recovery-Cycle Vertical-Slice Qualification Contract

Series 33 may claim the second recovery cycle is qualified only when:

1. The Series 32 frozen baseline passes all native audits.
2. Cycle two has a content-derived identity linked to the original cycle, reopened incident, frozen segment, and exact freeze head.
3. Recovery-authority and witness policies are cycle scoped and supplied or pinned by the verifier.
4. No cycle-one signature, checkpoint, certification, quarantine release, or closure can authorize cycle two.
5. Unresolved quarantines carry forward unless explicitly and authentically changed.
6. Candidate branches are anchored to the freeze and selected through one exact dual-quorum plan.
7. Cycle activation and branch selection commit atomically.
8. Re-entry requires a strictly later checkpoint and verified continuity under cycle-two policy.
9. Operational closure is a separate dual-quorum decision after accepted certification.
10. Multi-cycle history remains append-only and globally ordered.
11. Native and independent vectors agree on lineage, replay, policy, and receipt cases.
12. Race, rollback, abandonment, and resource-limit corpora pass.
13. The slice ends at closure and does not imply publication resumption.

This contract does not claim unlimited recoverability, universal branch canonicality, signer independence, or restoration of original trust.
