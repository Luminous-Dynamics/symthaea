Synthetic spacecraft communications design source - revision v0.1
Mission function: maintain authorized command and telemetry exchange with a ground endpoint.
Component: comms_transceiver
Interface: ground_comm_link
Requirement: REQ-COMM-001 - an unavailable radio link must be detectable within one simulator step.
Assumption: ASSUMP-COMM-UPLINK-AVAILABLE-AT-START - the link is available before event injection.
Fault mode: FM-COMM-001 - an injected radio blackout suppresses link availability.
Expected observation: ground_link_available=false during the declared blackout; orbital line-of-sight state does not change.
Monitor: MON-COMM-001 - assert RADIO_BLACKOUT_SUSPECTED within one step; LOS-only loss is a negative control.
Mitigation: MIT-COMM-001 - buffer noncritical telemetry and retry after link recovery.
Safety invariants: INV-COMMAND-AUTHORITY; INV-POWER-BUDGET.
