# Statistical Qualification Evidence

Series 53 prevents repeated copies of one easy test from being mistaken for broad safety evidence.

A campaign must cover eight explicit strata: anthropometry, environment, payload and load, power and thermal, timing and transport, injected faults, endurance, and human factors. Each trial carries unique artifact evidence, exposure time, execution independence, and blinding status.

Release evaluation requires:

- minimum total trials and exposure;
- minimum coverage in every stratum;
- minimum independent and blinded fractions;
- zero severe failures;
- a bounded non-severe failure count;
- conservative upper confidence bounds for failure probability and failures per hour.

The statistical report deliberately keeps `human_worn_authorized` false. Statistical sufficiency is one input to an external safety case, not self-authorization.
