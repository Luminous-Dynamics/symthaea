# Independent Recovery Proofs

A fault signal disappearing is not sufficient to clear a latched stop. A recovery proof binds the complete cleared fault set and required physical inspections to the exact boot epoch, fault epoch, challenge, deployment, calibration, journal head, and prior latch.

Proofs are authenticated, single-use, and require distinct operator and independent-safety approvals. A verified proof permits re-entry to startup only. It does not restore consent, arming, a prior session, or actuation authority.
