# Series 54 Release Gates

Series 54 is a closure series. It adds no authority-expanding mechanism.

A repository candidate must demonstrate all of the following:

1. A primary learned controller and independently implemented monitor controller agree within bounded direction, magnitude, impedance, freshness, identity, and software-diversity limits.
2. Enabled actuator frames arrive only from the authenticated local real-time controller. Remote diagnostics, maintenance, and independent safety ingress remain non-actuating.
3. Evidence artifacts are authenticated and bound to the exact release, source tree, deployment, hardware, calibration, and SBOM under review.
4. Human and cognitive data processing is purpose-bound, pseudonymous, minimally retained, revocable, and incapable of exporting raw biometric or cognitive-state data.
5. Qualification evidence covers every required stratum with unique artifacts, sufficient exposure, independent and blinded execution, zero severe failures, and conservative upper confidence bounds.
6. The evidence runner reports `human_worn_authorized = false`.

Repository gates do not replace compiled test evidence, independent safety review, regulated risk management, HIL and bench campaigns, environmental and EMC qualification, endurance testing, or supervised human-factors trials.
