# Degraded-Mode Orchestration

Every safety supervisor remains independently authoritative. The orchestrator takes the minimum fresh authority ceiling and the most restrictive stop state. Missing required domains, invalid samples, replay, and expiry produce zero authority or a latched stop.

Authority reductions are immediate. Authority increases require stable evidence followed by explicit operator and independent-safety acknowledgement. The orchestrator never manufactures gait, clears a source fault, or bypasses the actuator-local interlock.
