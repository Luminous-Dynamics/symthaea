# Human–Machine Interface Load Safety

Actuator safety and tissue safety are separate claims. The interface-load supervisor observes each attachment site for:

- cuff pressure;
- tangential shear;
- attachment slip;
- skin-interface temperature;
- sensor freshness and calibration identity;
- left/right pressure asymmetry.

Warning conditions restrict only the affected joints and the system-wide ceiling remains the minimum of every joint. Hard pressure, shear, slip, heat, missing sensors, stale samples, sequence loss, or calibration mismatch latch a zero-authority stop.

The stop does not clear with time. Recovery requires fit re-verification, a skin check, and physical attachment re-securing. This subsystem is a conservative load-transfer monitor, not a medical diagnostic or a substitute for validated cuff design, pressure mapping, clinical oversight, or emergency-release hardware.
