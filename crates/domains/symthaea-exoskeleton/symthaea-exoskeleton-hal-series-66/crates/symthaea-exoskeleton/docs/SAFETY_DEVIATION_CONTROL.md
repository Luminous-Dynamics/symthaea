# Safety Deviation Control

Known gaps must remain visible. Every deviation is release-bound, hazard- and requirement-linked, time-limited, evidence-backed, independently approved, and paired with compensating controls.

Emergency isolation, actuation safety, human-factors gaps, and critical or catastrophic deviations are never temporarily waivable. Any accepted deviation caps deployment to hardware-in-loop or below, and no deviation authorizes powered human-worn use.
