# Research Bundles

A research bundle collects local text artifacts into one report-like object:

- manifest text
- result text
- audit or preflight text
- local receipt text
- non-cryptographic bundle fingerprint

Research bundles are useful for lab notes and release artifacts.

They are not signed. They are not cryptographic. They are not Mycelix source-chain entries.

A future Mycelix bridge can replace local fingerprints with real content-addressed receipts, signatures, source-chain entries, and peer validation.
