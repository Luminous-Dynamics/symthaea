# Subterranean Capability Campaign XII

## Autonomy Governance Over Time

Campaign XII extends guarded post-deployment learning into a durable policy-governance system. The campaign adds explicit lifecycle state, incident-triggered quarantine, replay-resistant fleet quarantine, controlled policy transfer, irreversible retirement, evidence retention, checkpoint continuity, runtime authority integration, certification requirements, and a self-consistent governance evidence bundle.

## Core safety result

A learned policy is no longer merely a checkpoint selected by the current process. It has a persistent identity, origin, lineage, lifecycle, incident history, evidence obligations, and terminal retirement semantics. Imported policies cannot become active by transfer alone, and a safety incident removes canary authority through the same control cycle’s governance state.

## Offline verification target

The campaign is designed to pass warning-denied type checking, the complete existing deterministic test suite, the five new governance contracts, Rust formatting, exact-tree patch reconstruction, and packaged checksum verification. Production qualification still requires the real workspace, cryptographic adapters, fleet transport authentication, operational retention policy review, and physical deployment evidence.
