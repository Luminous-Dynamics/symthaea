# Subterranean Capability Campaign XI — Guarded Post-Deployment Learning

Campaign XI adds bounded post-deployment adaptation without allowing learning to redefine safety, train on intervention frames as nominal behavior, or replace the deployed controller without independent evidence and approval.

## Patch scope

1. Bounded adaptation policy and learning modes.
2. Immutable-baseline distribution-shift detection.
3. Provenance-rich bounded adaptation replay evidence.
4. Zero-authority shadow candidate training.
5. Deterministic held-out emergency non-regression evaluation.
6. Split-role, hardware-backed promotion approval.
7. Bounded canary activation and automatic rollback.
8. Runtime baseline/candidate integration beneath all safety authorities.
9. Persistent learning epoch and canary checkpoint state.
10. Operational adaptation, shift, canary, and rollback evidence.
11. Canonical post-deployment learning requirements and traceability.
12. Deterministic adaptation acceptance contracts.
13. Release-blocking certification integration.
14. Self-consistent adaptation evidence bundles.
15. Current-frame shift preflight before canary authority selection.
16. Post-deployment learning protocol and explicit non-claims.
17. Mechanical formatting normalization.

## Safety boundary

The candidate has no actuator authority during shadow learning. During a canary it may supply only the nominal command, after current-frame distribution-shift preflight and before physical hazards, operator constraints, field envelopes, actuator isolation, and final-command invariants are applied.

The preserved baseline remains the rollback target throughout canary execution.

## Offline validation target

The campaign is gated on:

- warning-denied compilation against API-compatible stand-ins;
- complete deterministic unit-test execution;
- adaptation-specific certification contracts;
- formatting and whitespace checks;
- production panic-marker audit;
- clean incremental and full patch-series application;
- exact Git-tree reproduction.

## Remaining external qualification

Campaign XI does not establish that the training distribution is representative, the deterministic emergency curriculum is exhaustive, approvals are cryptographically authentic, or online adaptation is suitable for production machinery. Those claims require the complete workspace, physical HIL, preregistered statistical studies, external safety control, independent review, and authenticated deployment infrastructure.

The complete verification record is generated during artifact packaging.
