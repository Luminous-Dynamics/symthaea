# Symthaea Subterranean Capability Campaign XIV

**Theme:** Ecological and civic stewardship underground  
**Date:** 2026-07-20  
**Baseline:** hardened v13 / patches 1–163

## Objective

Campaign XIV closes the gap between asset lifecycle safety and stewardship of the
living, cultural, and civic world around an underground operation. Previous campaigns
could determine whether the machine, team, mission, evidence, and site closure state
were internally safe. This campaign asks whether a candidate action remains within an
externally supplied environmental baseline, protected-zone authority, community
mandate, cumulative impact budget, and restoration obligation.

## Patch sequence

### 164. Add site-bound environmental baseline contracts

Adds deployment-bound groundwater, settlement, contamination, discharge, and receptor
limits with strict validation.

### 165. Enforce ecological and cultural exclusion zones

Adds buffered protected-depth zones for heritage, sacred, ecological, community, and
utility constraints.

### 166. Account for groundwater drawdown and discharge quality

Adds cumulative dewatering/discharge accounting and requires current external quality
evidence before discharge is treated as acceptable.

### 167. Account for contamination conservatively

Adds material-sensitive estimated and measured uncontained-mass ledgers that never
silently subtract conservative evidence.

### 168. Model cumulative excavation and subsidence

Adds excavation, support, and settlement accounting with pre-limit derating, return,
and stabilization states.

### 169. Track evidence-backed restoration obligations

Adds bounded restoration obligations whose completion requires both physical progress
and attributable external evidence.

### 170. Enforce replay-resistant community mandate scope

Adds site-bound mandate identity, validity, activity/depth/disturbance scope, public
evidence references, independent roles, epoch ordering, and replay rejection.

### 171. Compose stewardship authority

Combines groundwater, contamination, subsidence, protected zones, restoration, and
mandate scope into one safety-monotonic supervisor.

### 172. Expose explicit mission mapping and fail-closed assessment

Maps stewardship states to bounded probing, return, hold, and restoration intent.

### 173. Persist stewardship in operational checkpoint schema v7

Makes environmental and civic authority restart-safe and rejects invalid state before
activation.

### 174. Preserve inward escape at protected boundaries

Corrects protected-zone command semantics so deeper movement is prohibited while safe
inward retreat remains available.

### 175. Integrate stewardship into runtime actuation

Projects candidate environmental consequences before recovery planning and updates
actual ledgers only from the command that executed.

### 176. Record operational stewardship evidence

Adds groundwater, contamination, settlement, zone, mandate, restoration, and aggregate
stewardship metrics to the bounded evidence plane.

### 177. Expose atomic deployment installation

Adds deployment APIs for installing one complete validated stewardship supervisor and
reading its current assessment.

### 178. Preserve lifecycle priority and bounded protected probing

Full-suite review found that stewardship fallback could overwrite terminal
lifecycle/decommission evidence and that buffered approach had become an excessive
hold. The hardening patch preserves terminal lifecycle priority and restores a
strictly bounded probe-only approach.

### 179. Bind mandate ledgers to the installed site

Closes an identity gap by requiring the mandate ledger, protected-zone ledger, and
environmental baseline to share the same site and deployment identity.

### 180. Add stewardship release contracts

Adds deterministic contracts for protected approach, protected entry, groundwater
quality, contamination, subsidence, mandate replay, restoration evidence, and site
identity.

### 181. Add canonical requirements and traceability

Adds `SUB-EST-001` through `SUB-EST-006` and `SUB-CIV-001`, with complete links into
the canonical traceability matrix.

### 182. Make stewardship release blocking

Integrates the stewardship validator into the certifiable-autonomy release validator.

### 183. Add community-verifiable evidence bundles

Binds live supervisor state, recomputed assessment, validation, public evidence,
canonical requirements, and distinct authenticated community/environmental observers.

### 184. Define the ecological and civic stewardship protocol

Documents authority ordering, trust boundaries, evidence semantics, non-claims, and
production qualification requirements.

### 185. Publish the campaign plan

Records the ordered patch campaign and its internal safety argument.

### 186. Publish verification and normalize formatting

Records warning-denied compilation, full tests, exact-tree reconstruction, static
audits, checksums, and isolated nonfunctional formatting.

## Internal software claims

Campaign XIV establishes that:

1. candidate impacts are evaluated before actuation rather than only after damage;
2. protected-zone approach is probe-only and prohibited entry preserves inward escape;
3. missing water-quality evidence fails closed without disabling emergency dewatering;
4. conservative contamination and settlement accounting cannot be erased by a later favorable frame;
5. mandate authority is site-bound, role-separated, fresh, and replay resistant;
6. restoration cannot be declared complete from paperwork alone or from physical progress alone;
7. stewardship restrictions survive restart and appear in the same evidence record as the final command;
8. community-facing bundles cannot reuse one observer for both required independent roles or trust a stale cached assessment.

These are deterministic software claims. Environmental legitimacy, consent,
calibrated impact prediction, cryptographic authenticity, and regulatory approval
remain external responsibilities.
