Muse

A composition engine that writes music the way composers do — with hooks worth remembering, forms that argue, and judgment about what deserves to survive. Part of the Symthaea project.

The provenance, first

Muse contains no melody it learned from anyone. The composer is symbolic music theory in Rust — scales, voice leading, counterpoint, form — so every note has a reason you can ask for. The instrument samples are CC0 (VCSL/VSCO2). The expressive performance model is fitted on the research-licensed MAESTRO corpus, with provenance embedded in every export. When Muse hands you a piece, it is yours: a MIDI file you can open in any DAW, with no one else's music inside it.

Listen

Every piece below was composed deterministically from a seed — same seed, same piece, forever. Recorded through a soundfont render; the symbolic score is the real artifact.

Tango

The melody is born from the dance: dotted calls and wide reaches over a habanera whose accent hierarchy the bass locks to. The phrase rhetoric is declamatory — statement, interruption, answer, sharp stop.

Nocturne

The same engine, thinking differently: sighing stepwise hooks, suspensions that overstay into each cadence, arrivals that land softly and off the barline. Same seed as the tango above — a different musical identity, not a different dressing.

Passacaglia — persistence

Seven statements of a ground that remembers: stated, walked, filled, altered, restored as the climax, fragmented, and finally completed. The foundation is the piece's name, slowed into the bass.

Erosion Nocturne — an elegy

Persistence failing. The ground loses one tone per cycle while the melody continues above it. Near the end it nearly recovers — then the final return arrives missing the one tone that would carry it home, and the melody reaches for the tonic and stops one step short.

Lineage

The ground becomes its descendants: every cycle a recognizable child of the same ancestor — figured, transposed, never contour-flipped. Measured the most integrated form the engine has produced.

Fugue

Three voices, one subject: exposition with a real answer at the fifth, episodes on the subject's head, an inverted middle entry, a true stretto, and an augmented final entry — with species-checked counterpoint fitting the free voices to the theme.

Theme & Variations — a lullaby

Variation as structured remembering: theme, minore, figuration, and the theme returned — every section over the same harmonic skeleton. The ground never moves; only the surface.

Rondo — a memory arc

Every return of the theme but the last comes back wounded — shrunk, cut, ornamented, or incomplete, by the piece's emotional attitude. The final return alone is verbatim, lifted: finally complete.

How it gets better: hypotheses that are allowed to die

Muse improves through listening reviews and pre-registered experiments — deterministic tests, committed to the repository, that fail loudly when a hypothesis is wrong. Three that shaped the engine:

Species counterpoint should raise integration — falsified, twice

Making the fugue's verticals consonant measurably improved them (+25% on the consonance channel) and the integration score didn't move: the metric's bottleneck was temporal, not vertical. The falsification redirected the roadmap to the passacaglia — a form built on exactly the dimension the metric said mattered.

An invariant ground should out-integrate an anonymous bass — confirmed

Pre-registered against a control with identical rhythm, register, and density: the persistent ground won on every seed, up to 2×. The failed hypothesis above had predicted where to look.

Kinship should integrate like invariance — falsified three times, then confirmed with a law

Chains of contour flips lost. Drifting transpositions lost. Only when every generation descended from the ancestor by interval-preserving steps did lineage win — and then it beat strict invariance itself, 3×. The law the experiments left behind: kinship integrates in proportion to how much interval identity the transformation preserves.

What Muse knows about its own music

Every candidate the engine offers carries its measurements: an integration score (a spectral, minimum-cut analysis of how hard the piece is to pull apart), local and global coherence reported separately, and — for ground forms — a five-score judgment of whether the subject deserved to become a foundation at all. The measurements are observables, never fitness functions: the only thing Muse optimizes toward is what a listener keeps.

Where it runs

Locally. Muse Studio is a small web app served from your own machine: describe a feeling, audition genuinely different candidates (identities, not variations), and take away the MIDI you own. Nothing leaves the machine.
