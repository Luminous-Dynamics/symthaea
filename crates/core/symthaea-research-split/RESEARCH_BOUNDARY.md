# Scientific boundary

Passing this crate's software gates establishes that the declared split contract is implemented consistently. It does not establish that a chosen split is scientifically adequate.

In particular, `spatial-block=A` and `spatial-block=B` are identifiers, not evidence that the underlying regions are independent. A forward-time embargo is an enforced time separation, not proof that temporal dependence has decayed. Different acquisition identifiers are provenance facts, not proof of independent weather, illumination, orbit geometry, or preprocessing errors.

Any publication-quality experiment should therefore retain domain evidence for why its block geometry, buffer, grouping dimensions, and time embargo address the expected leakage mechanisms. Those claims should be preregistered where they affect primary analysis.
