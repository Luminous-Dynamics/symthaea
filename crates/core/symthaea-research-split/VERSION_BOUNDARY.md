# v1 boundary

The initial v1 contract is intentionally small: immutable assignments, explicit group separation, forward-time embargo, attributable separation evidence, digest verification, and fail-closed deserialization.

Future additions such as geospatial geometry, block generation, autocorrelation estimation, nested cross-validation, or spatial buffering should be introduced as separate reviewed tranches rather than silently changing the semantics of the v1 manifest.
