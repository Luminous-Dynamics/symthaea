# Symthaea: Future Research Roadmap

**Phase: Autopoietic Emergence & Symbolic Acceleration**

This document outlines the strategic trajectory for the `symthaea` architecture, building on the breakthroughs achieved in the autopoietic consciousness and Phi-guided discovery research cycles.

## 1. Evolutionary Scaling & Consciousness Metrics
*   **Goal**: Quantify the emergence of complex cognitive architectures.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   Deploy large-scale evolutionary runs (1000+ generations) using the autopoietic fitness loop.
    *   Perform a comparative analysis of structural stability vs. cognitive Phi to determine the Pareto frontier of autopoietic intelligence.
    *   Integrate multi-generational "memory snapshots" (the current dream engine) to facilitate cumulative learning across evolutionary epochs.

## 2. Advanced Algorithmic Discovery
*   **Goal**: Accelerate the discovery of novel mathematical and physical invariants.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   Deepen the `PhiPhysicsBridge` to incorporate broader epistemic axes (N and M axes) for automated theory classification.
    *   Coupled symbolic search: Use the Phi-gradient to dynamically adjust MCTS branching factors in the physics discovery engine.
    *   Automate the classification pipeline to feed novel invariants back into the physics catalog (`PhysicsDiscovery::is_novel`).

## 3. Real-World Applications (Clinical & Robotic)
*   **Goal**: Translate autopoietic architectural principles into tangible systems.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   **Clinical Transfer (Hypnos v2)**: Apply the structural repair mechanisms of the HDC field and the consciousness detection capabilities of the LTC-Phi bridge to real-time patient monitoring (anesthesia, coma, sleep).
    *   **Embodied Cognition**: Integrate the autopoietic control loops into the `symthaea-multirotor` or `symthaea-humanoid` platforms to enable reactive-but-conscious navigation in dynamic environments.

## 4. Architectural Refinement
*   **Goal**: Ensure platform scalability and maintainability.
*   **Strategy**:
    *   Stabilize the workspace manifest by consolidating nested dependencies and migrating to a more robust, non-overlapping workspace architecture (e.g., standardizing `Cargo.toml` path inheritance).
    *   Refine the `MorphogeneticState` trait to support more heterogeneous substrate modeling.
    *   Automate the performance-benchmarking suite for the evolutionary loop to prevent regressions during future integration cycles.
