\begin{abstract}
We present the \emph{Ramanujan Protocol}, a closed-loop pipeline for
automated mathematical conjecture that \textbf{observes} numerical data,
\textbf{discovers} formulas via genetic programming,
\textbf{verifies} them through Z3 SMT solving, and
\textbf{self-corrects} when findings are structurally trivial or
statistically unsupported.

The system's principal contribution is \emph{epistemic self-correction}:
it autonomously caught a GCT obstruction ``discovery'' that was a
structural tautology, and a ``universal power-law exponent'' for $abc$
triple quality that was an artifact of model choice (exponential decay
$R^2 = 0.93$ vs.\ power law $R^2 = 0.69$, AIC difference 596). Both
were retracted before publication.

As a test of cross-domain pattern matching, the system recovered all
7~known modularity correspondences between elliptic curves and modular
forms from raw coefficient data (95/95 prime matches, 0/3 decoy false
positives), confirmed the Sato--Tate $\sin^2\theta$ distribution from
302~primes, and computed BSD critical values matching LMFDB to
4~significant figures. It formally proved $R(3,3) = 6$ and $R(3,4) = 9$
via SAT encoding, and verified the Montgomery pair correlation against
GUE ($\chi^2 = 9.28$, $df = 19$, cannot reject at 5\%).

A spectral analysis of the cross-domain correlation matrix reveals
structural clustering: arithmetic sequences form one connected component
(Fiedler value $\lambda_2 = 0.273$) with Sato--Tate equidistribution as
a weakly connected outlier.

Implemented in Rust (5{,}400~LOC conjecture engine, 1{,}300~LOC number
theory module, 345+~tests, Z3 4.15.8 backend). Open source.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{Keywords:} automated conjecture, symbolic regression, formal
verification, self-correcting AI, epistemic integrity, cross-domain
pattern matching

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Automated conjecture generation has advanced significantly in recent
years, from Eureqa's physical law discovery~\citep{schmidt2009} to
DeepMind's knot theory insights~\citep{davies2021} and the Ramanujan
Machine's continued fraction identities~\citep{raayoni2021}. However,
existing systems share a critical weakness: \emph{they cannot catch
their own false positives}. A neural network that discovers a pattern
has no mechanism to audit whether that pattern is a structural artifact,
a model-fitting coincidence, or a genuine mathematical relationship.

We argue that trustworthy automated mathematics requires not just
discovery but \emph{epistemic self-correction}: the ability to detect
and retract findings that are structurally trivial or statistically
unsupported. We identify four requirements:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Grounded observation}: formulas discovered from computed
    data, not recalled from training corpora.
  \item \textbf{Formal verification}: conjectures proven by an
    independent engine (Z3 SMT solver), not just numerically validated.
  \item \textbf{Self-correction}: the system autonomously detects and
    retracts tautologies and model-dependent artifacts.
  \item \textbf{Cross-domain pattern matching}: the system finds
    correspondences between different mathematical domains without being
    told what to look for.
\end{enumerate}

The Ramanujan Protocol implements all four as a closed pipeline. We
demonstrate it across number theory, combinatorics, dynamical systems,
and Ramsey theory, with extended case studies in Langlands-type
correspondence recovery and extreme value statistics.

\section{Architecture}
\label{sec:architecture}

The pipeline consists of five stages:

\textbf{Stage 1: Observe.} Mathematical engines compute numerical
sequences: $a_p(E)$ from elliptic curve point counting, $p(n)$ from
partition enumeration, $\gamma_k$ from Riemann zeta zero finding, etc.
Each sequence carries domain metadata (NumberTheory, Physics,
Combinatorics).

\textbf{Stage 2: Discover.} Grammar-guided genetic programming with
ensemble search (3~seeds), template library seeding (growth-class
analysis), and semantic crossover (rejects offspring $10\times$ worse
than parents). Constants optimized by L-BFGS with finite-difference
gradients. Pareto-optimal (MSE, complexity) frontier computed alongside
scalar fitness.

\textbf{Stage 3: Verify.} Four verification levels:
(i)~numerical (held-out 20\% test split),
(ii)~bounded induction ($n \leq 1000$),
(iii)~formal Z3 proof (SMTLIB2 QF\_NIA/QF\_NRA, quantifier-free
nonlinear arithmetic),
(iv)~symbolic derivative verification ($dE/dt = 0$ for conservation
laws).

\textbf{Stage 4: Self-Correct.} Competing model analysis (AIC
comparison), bootstrap confidence intervals, structural tautology
detection. Any finding that fails these audits is retracted.

\textbf{Stage 5: Map.} Cross-domain correlation matrix computed via
Spearman rank correlation. Graph Laplacian eigenvalues extracted to
reveal the spectral geometry of mathematical interconnection.

\section{Results I: Formula Discovery and Proof}
\label{sec:results1}

\subsection{Closed-Loop Pipeline (Observe $\to$ Discover $\to$ Prove)}

The complete pipeline was demonstrated on triangular numbers: the engine
observed $\{1, 3, 6, 10, 15, \ldots\}$, discovered $T(n) = n(n+1)/2$
via GP, and Z3 proved the induction:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Base}: $T(1) = 1$ (Z3: UNSAT for negation).
  \item \textbf{Step}: $T(k+1) = T(k) + (k+1)$ (Z3: UNSAT for negation).
\end{itemize}
This constitutes a formal induction proof generated from raw numerical
data, with no human guidance on what formula to search for or how to
prove it.

Additional results: $\varphi$ from Fibonacci ratios
(MSE~$8.4 \times 10^{-21}$),
$E = x^2 + v^2$ conservation law (symbolic proof: $dE/dt = 2xv + 2v(-x) = 0$),
Bell--Stirling identity ($B(n) = \sum S(n,k)$, exact to $n=15$).

\section{Epistemic Self-Correction}
\label{sec:selfcorrect}

The Protocol's most distinctive capability is not discovery but
\emph{retraction}. The system autonomously caught two false positives:

\subsection{Structural Tautology: GCT Obstruction}

The engine computed Kronecker coefficient obstruction ratios for
same-weight partition triples and reported 99.9\% obstruction at
$n = 6$---apparently strong computational evidence. Post-hoc auditing
revealed this is a \textbf{mathematical tautology}: the
Littlewood--Richardson size constraint $|\mu|+|\nu|=|\lambda|$ forces
$2w = w$ for same-weight triples, so $w = 0$ and all non-trivial
triples vanish by construction. The finding was retracted before
publication, preventing a false claim of evidence for $P \neq NP$.

\subsection{Model Artifact: $abc$ Quality Decay}

The engine discovered $q(\text{rank}) \approx 1 + A/\text{rank}^B$
with exponent $B \approx 1.05$, stable across four scales ($c \leq$
1K--50K). This appeared to be a universal power law. Competing model
analysis revealed:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
  \toprule
  Model & $R^2$ & AIC & Verdict \\
  \midrule
  \textbf{Exponential}: $A \cdot e^{-B \cdot \text{rank}}$ & \textbf{0.930} & \textbf{$-865$} & \textbf{Best} \\
  Power law: $A/\text{rank}^B$ & 0.690 & $-270$ & Rejected \\
  Log-corrected: $A/(n \ln^C n)$ & 0.000 & $-469$ & Rejected \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The AIC difference of 596 is decisive. Bootstrap 95\% CI for the
power-law exponent: $B \in [0.92, 1.24]$---too wide to claim
$B \approx 1.05$. The true decay is exponential, consistent with
extreme value statistics.

\subsection{Significance}

These retractions are not failures---they are the system's primary
contribution. Most automated discovery systems report findings and move
on. The Ramanujan Protocol audits its own outputs via competing model
analysis (AIC), bootstrap confidence intervals, and structural
constraint checking. A system that cannot catch its own false positives
cannot be trusted with its true positives.

\section{Case Study: Cross-Domain Correspondence Recovery}
\label{sec:langlands}

As a test of the Protocol's cross-domain pattern matching capability, we
applied it to the Langlands correspondence between elliptic curves and
modular forms.

\subsection{Modularity Correspondence Recovery}

We generated $a_p(E) = p + 1 - \#E(\mathbb{F}_p)$ for 7~Cremona curves
(conductors 11--21) and $c_n(f)$ for 7~weight-2 newforms via Hecke
multiplicativity. The engine was given all 14~sequences plus 3~decoy
forms with plausible but incorrect coefficients, \emph{without pairing
information}.

\textbf{Result}: 7/7 correct correspondences identified (95/95 prime
matches), 0/3 decoy false positives. The engine recovered all known
modularity correspondences from raw numerical data. This demonstrates
that the Protocol can detect exact coefficient identities across
mathematical domains without being told what to look for.

\subsection{Additional Verifications}

\textbf{Sato--Tate distribution}: Frobenius angles
$\theta_p = \arccos(a_p / 2\sqrt{p})$ for 302~primes on curve 11a1
confirm the $\sin^2\theta$ density shape.

\textbf{Montgomery pair correlation}: Zeta zero spacings tested against
GUE: $\chi^2 = 9.28$ ($df = 19$, 5\% critical $\approx 31.3$). Cannot
reject GUE at 5\% significance. Level repulsion confirmed.

\textbf{BSD critical values}: $L(11\text{a}1, 1) = 0.2538$ (LMFDB:
0.2538, 4-digit match). All rank-0 curves satisfy $L(E,1) > 0$.

\section{Exploratory: Correlation Structure of Arithmetic Sequences}
\label{sec:spectral}

We computed Spearman rank correlations between six arithmetic sequences
(prime gaps, $abc$ quality, partitions, class numbers, zeta zero
spacings, Sato--Tate angles; $n = 100$ each) and analyzed the
resulting graph via spectral methods.

\subsection{Correlation Structure}

Sato--Tate angles are statistically independent of all other sequences
($|\rho| < 0.1$), confirming equidistribution and serving as a
methodological control. The remaining five sequences form a correlated
block with $|\rho| > 0.5$ for several pairs (e.g., class numbers $\leftrightarrow$
partitions: $\rho = 0.62$).

\subsection{Laplacian Spectrum}

The 6$\times$6 correlation matrix (absolute values as edge weights)
yields Graph Laplacian eigenvalues:
\[
  \lambda = \{0.000, \; 0.273, \; 1.860, \; 2.127, \; 2.716, \; 3.578\}
\]

$\lambda_1 = 0$: all mathematics is \textbf{one connected component}.
$\lambda_2 = 0.273$ (Fiedler value): the connection is \textbf{weak}
(spectral gap $\lambda_2/\lambda_6 = 0.076$).

\subsection{The Fiedler Clustering}

The Fiedler vector (eigenvector of $\lambda_2$) partitions the sequences:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrl}
  \toprule
  Sequence & Fiedler component & Cluster \\
  \midrule
  Sato--Tate & $+0.912$ & \textbf{A (isolated)} \\
  Prime gaps & $-0.195$ & B (arithmetic) \\
  $abc$ quality & $-0.196$ & B \\
  Partitions & $-0.196$ & B \\
  Class numbers & $-0.161$ & B \\
  Zeta spacings & $-0.166$ & B \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\textbf{Interpretation}: Arithmetic sequences (primes, partitions, class
numbers, $abc$ triples, zeta zeros) form one tightly coupled continent.
Sato--Tate equidistribution is a separate island. The Langlands
correspondence is the bridge between them---it maps the arithmetic
structure of elliptic curves to the equidistributed angles of their
Frobenius elements.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The Ramanujan Protocol demonstrates that automated conjecture generation
becomes trustworthy when paired with formal verification and epistemic
self-correction. The system's principal contribution is not any specific
formula it discovered, but the demonstration that an automated pipeline
can:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Retract} its own false positives (GCT tautology, $abc$
    model artifact) before publication.
  \item \textbf{Recover} known cross-domain correspondences (modularity,
    7/7 correct with 0 false positives) from raw data.
  \item \textbf{Verify} formally (Z3 induction proofs, Ramsey SAT)
    rather than just numerically.
\end{itemize}

The retractions are as important as the recoveries. A system that cannot
catch its own false positives cannot be trusted with its true positives.

The pipeline is open-source (Rust, 345+ tests) and implemented as part
of the Symthaea cognitive architecture.\footnote{%
  \url{https://github.com/Luminous-Dynamics/symthaea}}
