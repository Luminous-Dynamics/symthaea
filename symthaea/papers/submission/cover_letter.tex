\opening{Dear Editors,}

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, ``\textbf{Hyperdimensional Active Inference for Consciousness-Aware Distributed Intelligence},'' for your consideration.

\textbf{Summary.} This paper introduces Symthaea, a novel architecture that unifies four independently validated paradigms---Hyperdimensional Computing (HDC), Integrated Information Theory (IIT), Liquid Time-Constant Networks (LTC/CfC), and Active Inference---into a single coherent framework for consciousness-aware distributed intelligence. To our knowledge, this is the first system to combine all four paradigms, placing it in a unique ``blue ocean'' with no direct competitors.

\textbf{Key contributions:}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Unified architecture} integrating HDC (16,384-dimensional holographic vectors), IIT ($\Phi$ proxy with Spearman $\rho = 0.50$ against exact computation), LTC/CfC (real-time neural dynamics at 59K--87K inferences/sec), and Active Inference (7.9$\times$ speedup over pymdp).
    \item \textbf{Consciousness-aware federated learning} with Byzantine fault tolerance validated to 34\%, epistemic-weighted aggregation, and differential privacy with R\'{e}nyi accounting.
    \item \textbf{16 validated benchmarks} across neuroscience, ethics, speech, vision, and distributed systems, including 92.9\% on the ETHICS benchmark (HDC-only, no LLM), 94.5\% speaker identification, and biologically-grounded \textit{C.~elegans} connectome analysis (448 neurons, 7,379 connections).
    \item \textbf{Open-source implementation} in Rust ($\sim$338K LOC, 3,200+ tests), demonstrating that consciousness-inspired computation is both theoretically grounded and practically viable.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Novelty.} While Liquid AI (LFM2/LFM2.5) validates LTC commercially and FedHDC demonstrates federated HDC, no existing system combines all four paradigms. Our HDC moral algebra achieves 92.9\% on ETHICS without any language model, using only 16,384-dimensional vectors with compositional operators---a fundamentally different approach to ethical reasoning.

\textbf{Reproducibility.} All benchmarks are reproducible via standalone Rust examples. Results files, supplementary reproduction instructions, and the complete codebase are included.

We believe this work is well-suited for Neural Computation given its interdisciplinary scope spanning computational neuroscience, distributed systems, and AI safety.
