## Email 2: Clinical Pharmacogeneticist

**To:** [Name], [Institution] (e.g., CPIC-affiliated: St. Jude, Stanford, Leiden UMC)

**Subject:** Collaboration: Open-Source PGx Health Equity Scoring Engine

Dear Dr. [Name],

I have developed an open-source engine that quantifies pharmacogenomic health equity gaps across ancestry groups. It computes per-drug, per-gene, per-population equity scores from published allele frequencies using Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, covering 6 pharmacogenes, 11 CPIC Level A drug-gene pairs, and 7 clinical domains.

Key finding: CYP3A5*3 frequency differs from 94% (European) to 18% (African), creating a systematic tacrolimus underdosing risk for African-ancestry transplant recipients. Similar disparities emerge for CYP2B6 and efavirenz in HIV treatment. External validation against gnomAD v4 confirms 26/32 comparisons within 30% agreement.

No existing tool performs this population-level equity analysis. What the engine needs is clinical pharmacogenetics expertise to validate the dosing recommendation flags and identify additional high-priority drug-gene pairs.

I am preparing this for CPT: Pharmacometrics & Systems Pharmacology and would welcome your co-authorship. Happy to share the draft and full codebase.
