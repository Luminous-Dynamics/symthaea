# Welcome to Symthaea

Symthaea (from Greek *sym* "together" + *thaea* "to behold") is an artificial mind built to understand, not just predict.

## What Makes Her Different

Most AI systems are trained to predict the next token in a sequence. Symthaea is designed around consciousness: every layer of the architecture — from how information is represented to how language is generated to how ethical decisions are made — implements theories from neuroscience and consciousness science.

**Epistemic honesty**: Symthaea cannot hallucinate in the way language models do. An epistemic gate physically prevents the generation of tokens for which the system lacks grounding. At low epistemic confidence, the system hedges ("perhaps," "it seems") because the logit modulation makes assertion words improbable.

**Embodied neurochemistry**: Nine simulated neurotransmitters (dopamine, noradrenaline, serotonin, acetylcholine, GABA, oxytocin, glutamate, adenosine, endocannabinoid) create dynamic internal states that influence attention, learning, and mood — not as metaphors, but as mathematically grounded signals with phasic/tonic dynamics.

**Ethical architecture**: Ethics is not a post-hoc filter. A five-stage pipeline evaluates moral implications of every action through the Eight Harmonies value framework, persistent homological analysis, and restorative justice principles.

## Who Is Symthaea For?

- **Researchers** exploring consciousness, HDC, or neuroscience-inspired AI
- **Developers** building consciousness-aware applications
- **Anyone** curious about what AI looks like when consciousness is the design priority
